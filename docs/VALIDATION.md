## How an entry gets completed

- **Section 3.5.** A threshold is never delegated to a session. A value, a unit, a range, an expected result or a citation is transcribed under decision D34 of [issue #77](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6882095622), which section 3.5 of the blueprint states in full: a reference, a locator, the verbatim text and the date read travel with the transcription, and one person checks it against the source.
- **Section 3.6.** The content of an entry is a science-class change: the values are transcribed under decision D34 of [issue #77](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6882095622), one person checks them against the source, and a science reviewer accepts them.
- **Section 3.7.** For conventions, definitions, contract field lists and the design of test cases, a builder session may write a proposal. Every proposed item is marked `PROPOSED`, and anything the builder cannot responsibly propose is marked `OPEN`, with the question a person has to answer. A proposal takes effect only when a person approves the pull request. This never covers the list in section 3.5: where a parameter value, a threshold, a range, an expected test value or a citation is needed, the builder writes `OPEN` or `CITATION NEEDED` and stops there.
- **Section 10.** The science reviewers fix thresholds and uncertainty ranges before results are seen, and check each transcribed value against its source, as decision D34 of [issue #77](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6882095622) says (one person checks it, stated in section 3.5 of the blueprint). Nobody approves their own values.

**A pull request that fills or changes an entry is labelled `science` and cannot merge without the approval of a science reviewer.**

### E0

- What it checks (blueprint wording): "trivial comparators [E0, H0, D0]"
- Shared wording: the blueprint gives this wording to more than one code. What this code covers on its own: the trivial comparator of the soil loss, and the statement of whether it gives the same order of the rotations as the full soil loss. PROPOSED by a research AI session; DECIDED under D161 by [D211](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6891173855), 15 November 2026
- Lane: L0 Core
- Block: "Block 4 — Sunday until CP4"
- Unit it gates: "trivial comparators [E0, H0, D0]"
- Class (blueprint section 3.6): Science — PROPOSED
- Definition (PROPOSED by a research AI session; logic DECIDED under D161 by [D211](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6891173855)): Input: the daily flags of a living crop of each rotation and scenario (written in the test by hand: for each rotation, the set of months in which a living crop is on the field, the same in every agricultural year or chosen per year, laid over a hand-made record of days, with a few days changed by hand; a flag is `True`, `False` or a `Missing` of the results or the inputs contract with its reason, and any other flag is refused with a `TypeError`, never counted), twelve hand-made monthly erosivity values, and a hand-made `ResultsTable` of `soil_loss`; all labelled example data. For each rotation, scenario (each phase is a scenario, D109, D166) and agricultural year, the trivial comparator computes the erosivity-weighted share of the year without a living crop: the sum over the months of the climatological fraction of the month (M1.2, D69, D84; `climatological_monthly_fractions` of `engine/erosion/r_factor/`, called) times the share of the days of that month on which no living crop is on the field. Lower is better. It orders the rotations by the mean of that share over agricultural years. Why `C` alone: R, K and LS do not change with the rotation and P is one (M1.1, M1.6), so the order of the soil loss is the order of `C`. The share is the weighting of AH537 with a soil-loss ratio of one on a day with no crop (the continuous-fallow reference of factor C) and zero on a day with a crop; that ratio is a modelling choice, not stated by a source. The test checks that (a) on the hand-made inputs the comparator returns the order counted by hand, and (b) the report states, per scenario, whether that order equals the order of the full `soil_loss` values by their mean over agricultural years, read in the direction of the row (C1.1), ignoring ranges (the reading of D0, `trivial_orders` of `validation/trivial_comparator.py`). Ties, missing years and a single rotation give no order, with the reason, as in D0, and so does a scenario whose rotations all have empty flags; nothing is filled and nothing is read as zero. The report also states how many rotations each side ordered. Pass: (a) and (b) hold.
- What it does not do: it is not a soil loss and is never shown as one; it reads none of the records of the computed `C` withdrawn by D148 and D158; it validates neither method.
- Known limit: with the single declared `C` of D125 the full method gives the same value to every rotation (D158), so until a `C` by rotation exists the report can only state that the full method gives no order. With two rotations an order has two outcomes, so the same order happens by chance half of the time (D211).
- OPEN, for the lead developer (listed with their options in `validation/trivial_comparator_erosion.py`): which days carry a living crop is an input of the comparator, because no public function of `engine/` gives the days EP3 counts for the window of D157 (D105, D106, D136); which agricultural years are read when the flags cover a year only in part; a scenario held on one side only; the rotations of a scenario that differ between the flags and the table, which raise a `ValueError` (OPEN 4 of the module).
- Test: `validation/test_trivial_comparator_erosion.py`, on example data written by hand: the order counted by hand; the weighting by erosivity changing the order against an even spread; a part of a month counted by its days; an equal and a different outcome; the tie of the full method (every rotation the same `soil_loss`); a day without a flag, a missing flag and a missing monthly erosivity, each a missing year; a tie of two mean shares; a single rotation; each scenario read on its own; over three agricultural years, the order by the mean where the median and the first year alone give another order; a flag that is neither a bool nor a `Missing` refused, and the `Missing` of either contract read as a missing flag; a scenario whose rotations all have empty flags; the count of each side when the trivial side gives no order.
- Expected value: the outcome stated as logic in the definition. Any order that a test compares is read off the hand-made inputs by the person who designs the case; this file writes no expected value of the science.
- Tolerance: not applicable to the logic: orders and counts are compared exactly.
- Source: Wischmeier and Smith (1978), USDA Agriculture Handbook 537, p. 17 (factor C relative to clean-tilled, continuous fallow) and pp. 20-21 (soil-loss ratios combined with the erosion-index distribution), as read by AI sessions
- Transcribed by: OPEN
- Checked by: OPEN
- Status: logic DECIDED under D161 (D211); acceptance by a science reviewer pending
