# Water entry point

Unit `l1/water-ep3-parameters` (issue #153): entry point EP3 of `contracts/ENTRYPOINTS.md`, `compute_water_deficit_exposure`, in `engine/water/exposure.py`, exported by `engine/water/__init__.py`. It replaces the mechanical form of unit `l1/water-entrypoint` (issue #134). The lookup of a parameter lives in `engine/water/_parameters.py`.

Unit `l1/water-ep3-identifiers` (issue #191) makes EP3 read every parameter of the water records drafted for the lead developer's check (outside the repository; their ids, scopes and units only, never their values): the basal crop coefficient by crop and growth stage (FAO-56 Table 17, D78), the maximum root depth and the depletion fraction by crop (Tables 22, D78), the depth of the evaporation layer `Ze` and the two constants of the total evaporable water (D77), and the two bounds of PC-4 (D82) passed to `et0/` as `Et0Bounds`.

Unit `l1/water-crop-stages` (issue #206) gives EP3 the growth stages of each crop period. `engine/water/stages.py` places the four FAO-56 stages (initial, development, mid, late) end to end from the start of each calendar component (its sowing), using four stage lengths in days read from `parameters` by crop (`fao56_stage_length_<stage>`, PROPOSED). The basal crop coefficient of a crop day is then taken from its stage, with the straight line between the values of Table 17 that FAO-56 uses to build the crop coefficient curve (Eq. 66, form PROPOSED), and the root depth grows by stage from a new `initial_root_depth` to `maximum_root_depth` (D78, provisional curves, form PROPOSED). A crop period whose stage lengths are missing keeps the behaviour of D79: its days are missing with the reason, never guessed. A period the lengths do not fill exactly is missing too (OPEN 25). EP2 does not change (D56). See choices 6, 16 and 17.

Unit `l1/water-fallow-residue` (issue #217) builds the fallow day of decisions D110, D111, D115 and D116 of #77. A fallow day is a day of the calendar on which no component carries a crop. On it, the fallow is weed-free (D111, declared as an assumption): no crop reaches the balance, so nothing is transpired. The surface layer takes the cover fraction `fc` and the wetted fraction `fw` from two records of a day with no crop (D110 gives `fc = 0`, `fw = 1`), and the total evaporable water is reduced by the surface residue cover of the fallow, "5 % for each 10 %" of the surface covered (D110, FAO-56 chapter 11, form PROPOSED), with the readily evaporable water not above it. The cover is one record per fallow, read by the crop sown after the fallow (D115: the cover at sowing), and it is the one record both engines read (D116). `engine/water/fallow.py` holds the three pure steps. Days with a crop are computed exactly as before. See choices 18 to 22 and OPEN 30 to 35.

> **Status: PROPOSED** (blueprint section 3.7). The order of the checks, the reason code of each item, the identifier and scope of each parameter, how the crop of a day is read from the calendar, the format of every `detail`, the choices under [PROPOSED choices](#proposed-choices) and the test cases below were written by a builder session. They take effect only when a person approves the pull request. Items under [OPEN](#open) need an answer from a person.

**What this unit does.** For every day of the climate record it chains the four internal parts of the water engine of D44: the reference evapotranspiration (`et0/`), the runoff by the Curve Number (`runoff/`), the evaporation coefficient `Ke` from the surface-layer balance (`evaporation/`) and the one-layer daily balance (`balance/`). Every constant and parameter of those parts is read from the argument `parameters` by its `id` and `scope`. The daily chain is reduced to one item per agricultural year of the record (D47).

**What it returns is never a number.** Convention C1.4 says that while the definition of the indicator is incomplete (the critical period, which quantities of the balance are water transpired and water demanded), no engine reports `water_deficit_exposure` as anything but missing. So a year whose daily balance was computed on every day is missing with `NOT_AVAILABLE` and a detail saying the definition is incomplete (D61); every other year is missing with the reason that applies. The four parts are called, not changed. This folder holds no parameter value: not in the code, not in this file. The numbers of the test file are PLACEHOLDER, plainly not real.

Acceptance test, from the repository root:

```bash
py -3.12 -m pytest engine/water validation
```

## Signature

```text
compute_water_deficit_exposure(
    calendar: RotationCalendar,
    climate: SiteClimate,
    soil_slope: SiteSoilSlope,
    *,
    parameters: dict[str, dict],
) -> list[WaterDeficitExposure]
```

As EP3 is written in `contracts/ENTRYPOINTS.md` since #144 (Q3, D46, D56). `parameters` is passed by keyword only and has no default (G3). It is the catalogue exactly as `params.catalogue.load_folder` returns it for `params/records/`: every record, keyed by the path of its file. The models are imported only as `contracts.inputs.<module>` and `contracts.results.models`; `Missing` is the one of `contracts/results/models.py`; no new missing shape is defined.

The entry point reads a record only through the pure readers of `params.catalogue`: `identity` to find it by `id` and `scope`, and `read_value` to read it (G1). It never calls `load_folder` and reads no file. `read_range` is not used: no range is computed, because one call is one scenario and no indicator value is a number (Q6).

`soil_slope` is checked (G3, G5) and not read: no part reads a soil property yet. The soil's available water of D49 is read as the parameter `column_capacity` when a record exists (OPEN 7).

## What it returns

Always a list with at least one item, and no item is a number. Every item keeps `indicator`, `direction` and `unit` (C3.1), read from `contracts/results/models.py`. A missing item carries no provenance, no source, no range and no `range_missing` (the model forbids them).

| Case | What is returned |
|---|---|
| `calendar.ag_year_start.month` is missing | One item, `time_basis: "rotation"`, reason `MISSING_INPUT`, detail names the start month |
| `climate.daily_series` has no `precipitation` series | One item, `time_basis: "rotation"`, reason `MISSING_INPUT`, detail names `precipitation` |
| Otherwise | One item per agricultural year of the `precipitation` series (D47), `time_basis: "ag_year"`, with its `ag_year`, each missing with the first reason of the table below |

Reason of each yearly item, the first row that applies:

| Situation | Reason | Convention |
|---|---|---|
| The calendar is infeasible (`feasibility.feasible` is false) | `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, every year; the chain does not run | G4, first row |
| The daily series the water engine reads that are present do not share one time zone | `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, every year; the chain does not run | C6.3; G4, sixth row |
| A series the water engine reads that is present does not cover the year from its first day to its last | `NO_COVERAGE`; detail names each such series | C5.5 |
| A series, or the latitude or elevation of the point, arrives in a unit string the parts do not accept | `MISSING_INPUT` (PROPOSED, Q11); detail names each one and both unit strings; the chain does not run | C1.1; G4, seventh row |
| The daily chain has no value on a day of the year (the storage at the end of a balance day is missing) | The reason of the first such day: `MISSING_INPUT`, or `NOT_COMPUTABLE` when a part refused a value; detail below | G4, second to fourth rows and the two rows of a parameter; C3.3 |
| The daily balance was computed on every day of the year | `NOT_AVAILABLE`, detail: the definition of the indicator is incomplete (C1.4) | D61 |

The daily series the water engine reads are `precipitation` (D23) and the five inputs of the reference evapotranspiration, read from `CLIMATE_QUANTITIES` of `engine/water/et0/quantities.py`. The unit string of each is the one the parts transcribed from `contracts/CLIMATE_QUANTITIES.md` (`PRECIPITATION_UNIT` of `runoff/`, `ACCEPTED_UNITS` of `et0/`). The point is accepted in the unit strings D55 sets for the climate adapter: `degree` for latitude, `m` for elevation.

### The daily chain

The chain runs from the first day that every series it reads covers to the last day of the `precipitation` series, one day after the other, never resetting a state (D44). For each day:

1. **Reference evapotranspiration**: `reference_evapotranspiration_from_climate_with_bounds` of `et0/`, with `Et0Constants` and the two bounds of D82 (`Et0Bounds`, PC-4) built from the catalogue, `PointUnits` of D55, and a unit-guard code that is never emitted (units are checked before the chain runs).
2. **Crop of the day** (PROPOSED): the crop identifier of the one component of the calendar whose dates cover the day; no crop when none does; not known (`MISSING_INPUT`, naming Q5) for a day outside the dates of the calendar; not known (`MISSING_INPUT`) when a component has a missing date and no dated component covers the day; `NOT_COMPUTABLE` when two components cover the day. On a day with a crop, the three records of `basal_crop_coefficient` of the crop (one per stage) and every parameter read by crop (the table below: `maximum_root_depth`, `readily_available_fraction`, `initial_root_depth` and the four stage lengths) are read, so that a missing record is named. The basal coefficient and the root depth of the day come from `_growth_of_day` (choice 6): the stages of the component are placed from its start (`stages.place_stages`, choice 16), and each value is taken from the stage of the day (`stages.by_stage`, choice 17). The crop of the balance is built from them and `readily_available_fraction`. A stage length with no value makes the basal coefficient and the crop of the balance `MISSING_INPUT`, with a `chain` item naming the unknown stage and D79; stages that cannot be placed make them `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, with a `chain` item saying why; a record the stage of the day needs that is missing makes them `MISSING_INPUT`, naming it. The states carried from day to day stay missing after such a day.
   On a day with no crop (unit #217), the crop of the day is `None`: the basal coefficient, `crop_height` and `curve_number` are read with the scope of a day with no crop, no crop reaches the balance (D111), and the surface layer of the fallow is built as in step 4.
3. **Runoff**: `runoff_day` of `runoff/`, with the precipitation of the day, the curve number of the crop of the day (or of a day with no crop) and `CurveNumberConstants` from the catalogue.
4. **`Ke`**: the total evaporable water, computed once on the first day of the chain from the layer (`total_evaporable_water` of `evaporation/`, choice 8); the climate term of the upper limit (`climate_adjusted_upper_limit`, from the wind speed of the day, the minimum relative humidity and the crop height), the upper limit (`upper_limit_of_crop_coefficient`), the cover fraction (`cover_fraction_from_basal_coefficient`), then `step` of `evaporation/`, carrying the depletion of the surface layer from day to day. On a day with no crop (D110, unit #217) the upper limit and the cover fraction are not computed: `step` is given the cover fraction and the wetted fraction of the records of a day with no crop, and a total evaporable water reduced by the residue cover of the fallow (`fallow.mulched_total_evaporable_water`, read by the crop sown after the fallow, `fallow.following_crop`), with the readily evaporable water not above it (`fallow.readily_evaporable_water_not_above`). A depletion carried into that day above the reduced total is `NOT_COMPUTABLE` (choice 21, OPEN 30).
5. **Balance**: `step` of `balance/`, with the precipitation, the runoff, the reference evapotranspiration, the `Ke` of the surface layer and the crop of the day (`CropDay` from the catalogue, `None` on a day with no crop), carrying the storage from day to day.

The entry point calls the daily `step` of the surface layer and of the balance, not `run_surface_layer` and `run_balance`, so that a value one part refuses on one day makes that day missing instead of raising for the whole record. Once the state at the end of a day is missing, every later day starts from a missing state that names the first cause, exactly as the two `run_` functions carry it.

### Format of `detail` (PROPOSED)

English, for developers. A year the chain did not compute reads `water_deficit_exposure is not computed for agricultural year <Y>: the daily water balance has no value on <n> day(s) of the year, the first on <date> (<code>: <detail of that day>); <item>; <item>; ...`. The items, in this order:

| Item | When |
|---|---|
| `input MISSING_INPUT: the climate record has no daily series '<name>' (G4, third row)` | One per absent series |
| `input MISSING_INPUT: the daily series '<name>' holds <n> day(s) without a value in agricultural year <Y>, the first on <date>, and no gap is filled (G4, fourth row, C3.4)` | One per series with days held missing in the year |
| `input <code>: the <latitude or elevation> of the point of the climate record is missing: <its detail>` | One per missing value of the point the reference evapotranspiration reads; longitude is not read |
| `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameters with no record in parameters (D56), scope <scope>: '<id>', '<id>', ...` | One per scope, naming every parameter with no record that was first needed on or before the last day of the year |
| `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameter '<id>' with scope <scope>: the record holds no value: <reason of the record>` | One per record whose value is `null` |
| `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameter '<id>' with scope <scope>: the record is not read: <why>` | One per record that `read_value` refuses (its message), or per two records that share `id` and `scope` |
| `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameters that cannot be looked up: the record(s) '<key>', ... have no well-formed 'id' and 'scope', so they cannot be compared and could be records of this parameter (F3c of params/README.md); ...`, then `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameters that cannot be looked up, scope <scope>: '<id>', '<id>', ...` | When a record of `parameters` has no well-formed `id` and `scope`: the malformed records named once by their key, then the parameters grouped by scope, as above |
| `chain <code>: <detail>` | One per problem of the chain met on or before the last day of the year: a crop that is not known, two components on one day, a value a part refuses (`<part> refuses a value: <its message>`), a fallow with no crop after it (OPEN 31), a reduction of the total evaporable water the method does not compute, a depletion above the reduced total (OPEN 30) |
| `indicator NOT_AVAILABLE: the definition of the indicator is incomplete (C1.4) ... (decision D61 of issue #77)` | Always, last |

A scope is written as JSON text with sorted keys: `{}`, `{"crop": "<crop identifier>"}`, `{"management": "no_crop"}`, `{"crop": "<crop identifier>", "management": "no_crop"}`. The other details are one fixed text each: infeasible calendar, time zones, start month, `precipitation` absent, a year not covered (`the daily series '<name>', ... do not cover agricultural year <Y> from its first day to its last (C5.5)`), units not accepted (naming Q11 and each unit string), and a computed year (`... the daily water balance was computed on every day of the year, but the definition of the indicator is incomplete (C1.4) ... (decision D61 of issue #77)`).

## Parameters

PROPOSED: one identifier and one scope for each parameter the chain reads. Where a part's README table proposes an identifier it is used as it is; the identifiers of `et0/` and of the climate term of `evaporation/` are written there as `<group>.<field>`, and a record `id` holds no dot (`params/README.md`), so the dot is written as an underscore. The value of every row is a person's (D34); none is in this repository.

The scope column says how a parameter is looked up (PROPOSED; the words allowed under each scope key are question 4 of `params/README.md`, OPEN):

- `site`: the empty scope `{}`. One record for the field and the method.
- `crop or no crop`: `{"crop": "<crop_id>"}`, the `crop_id` of the calendar component of the day; on a day with no crop, `{"management": "no_crop"}`.
- `crop and stage, or no crop`: on a day with a crop, `{"crop": "<crop_id>", "stage": "<stage>"}` for each of the stage words `initial`, `mid` and `end` of the drafted records (PROPOSED; the words allowed under `stage` are question 4 of `params/README.md`); on a day with no crop, `{"management": "no_crop"}`.
- `crop`: `{"crop": "<crop_id>"}` only; read on a day with a crop.
- `no crop`: `{"management": "no_crop"}` only; read on a day with no crop (unit #217).
- `site, on a day with no crop`: the empty scope `{}`, read on the first day with no crop that needs it (unit #217).
- `no crop, by the crop that follows`: `{"crop": "<crop_id>", "management": "no_crop"}`, with the `crop_id` of the component sown first after the day; read on a day with no crop (unit #217, choice 19).

The lookup is exact: a record with the empty scope is never read in place of one that names a crop, nor the other way round (question 5 of `params/README.md`, OPEN).

## PROPOSED choices

Where EP3, the brief, the conventions and the decisions do not settle something, the option that invents least. Each is a proposal; the question is under [OPEN](#open).

1. **Time basis.** Yearly items are on `ag_year` (D47); the two cases where the years cannot be laid out give one item on `rotation`, as in #134. OPEN 1, OPEN 2.
2. **Precedence**: infeasible calendar, time zones, coverage, units, then the chain. OPEN 12.
3. **Scope words and exact lookup**, as above. OPEN 6.
4. **New identifiers**: `minimum_relative_humidity`, `column_capacity`, `column_depth`, `initial_storage`, `crop_height`, `curve_number`, `basal_crop_coefficient`, `readily_available_fraction`, `field_capacity_water_content`, `wilting_point_water_content`. Where a part has an argument of that name, the name is kept; `capacity` is written `column_capacity` so that it is not ambiguous in a catalogue shared with other engines. OPEN 6.
5. **Crop of a day from the calendar**, as in step 2 of the chain. Days outside the dates of the calendar have no known crop, because how the rotation is repeated over the years of the record is not decided (Q5). OPEN 5.
6. **A day with a crop takes its basal coefficient and root depth from its growth stage** (unit #206; it replaces the choice of unit #191, under which every crop day was missing). D78 gives the basal coefficient one value per growth stage (FAO-56 Table 17) and the root depth a crop-specific growth (FAO-56 Annex 8, provisional), and both need the stages. The calendar carries no growth stage and EP2 does not change (D56), so EP3 places them itself, per component, from the stage lengths of its crop (choice 16), and takes both values of each day from its stage (choice 17), in one function, `_growth_of_day`. On a day with a crop EP3 reads the three stage records of `basal_crop_coefficient` and every parameter by crop (a missing one is named). When a stage length has no value, D79 (USER-U) applies: missing stages stay missing for the water indicator they affect, so every day of that period is `MISSING_INPUT` naming the unknown stage and D79, and, as the surface layer and the balance carry their state, so is every later day. `crop_height`, `cover_fraction_exponent` and `curve_number` stay one value per crop (no record is drafted for them). OPEN 4.
7. **The minimum relative humidity is a parameter of the site.** The climate term of the upper limit needs it every day, and no daily series carries it: humidity travels as dew point (D25). OPEN 3.
8. **`total_evaporable_water` is computed from the surface layer** (unit `l1/water-ep3-identifiers`, issue #191): `total_evaporable_water` of `evaporation/` with the depth of the layer `Ze` (D77) and the two constants of its form, read from the catalogue, and the water contents at field capacity and wilting point of the layer, read as two parameters of the site because no soil property reaches the engine with an agreed identifier. It is computed once, on the first day of the chain. A result the part does not compute (`NOT_COMPUTABLE`), or an operand it refuses, is a `chain` item; a missing operand is named as a parameter. OPEN 13.
9. **The cover fraction of a day with a crop is computed from `Kcb`** (`cover_fraction_from_basal_coefficient`), not observed: no observed cover fraction reaches the engine. On a day with no crop it is the record of D110 (choice 18). OPEN 4.
10. **`Ke` reaches the balance as the surface layer gives it**, also on a day the surface layer runs short (OPEN 2 of `evaporation/README.md`). OPEN 11.
11. **Units are checked before the chain runs.** A series or a value of the point in another unit string makes every covered year `MISSING_INPUT`, naming it and Q11; nothing is converted. Because of that check, the unit-guard code passed to `et0/` and `runoff/` (`MISSING_INPUT`) is never emitted. The unit strings of the parameter records are not checked. OPEN 8, OPEN 9.
12. **A value a part refuses is `NOT_COMPUTABLE`.** The parts raise `ValueError` for a number outside what they accept (a negative depth, a storage above the capacity, a readily evaporable water above the total). That number comes from the catalogue or the climate record, not from a defect of the caller of EP3, so G5 does not let EP3 raise: the day is missing with `NOT_COMPUTABLE` (G4, "the method does not cover the case"), naming the part and its message, and later days start from a missing state. OPEN 10.
13. **The days of the chain** run from the first day every series it reads covers, to the last day of the `precipitation` series; `initial_storage` and `initial_depletion` are the states at the start of that day. A year before that day is `NO_COVERAGE` anyway, since a series does not cover its first day. OPEN 14.
14. **A record `read_value` refuses**, or two records with one `id` and `scope`, give `MISSING_INPUT` naming the parameter and why: the value the computation needs is not available to it (G4, second row). The runner passes only a catalogue that `check_catalogue` passed (G3), so this happens only if it does not. A record anywhere in `parameters` without a well-formed `id` and `scope` cannot be compared, so it could be a record of any parameter: it is reported, not skipped (F3c of `params/README.md`), and **every** parameter is `MISSING_INPUT`, naming it and the key of each such record, as EP1 does in `engine/erosion/_parameters.py` (#158).
15. **What a year needs.** A year is computed when the storage at the end of every one of its balance days is a number. A day with no crop counts like any other day; whether it counts for the indicator is C1.4 (OPEN).
16. **The stages of a crop period** (unit #206, `stages.place_stages`). The four stages follow one another from the start date of the component, its sowing: day one of the period is day one of the initial stage, and each stage begins the day after the one before it ends. Each length is read by crop (scope `{"crop": <crop_id>}`), once per component, on the first day of it the chain meets. A length must be a whole number of days of at least one, and the four must add up to the days of the period (from its start to its end, both inclusive); otherwise the whole period is `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, with a `chain` item naming the component and why, and no stage is guessed. FAO-56 does not say how to fit stage lengths to a period they do not fill (OPEN 25). The stage lengths depend on the crop only, not on the sowing date (OPEN 26).
17. **The value of a day by stage** (unit #206, `stages.by_stage`): constant in the initial and mid stages, and a straight line in the development and late stages, `start + (j / L) × (next − start)`, with `j` the day of the stage counted from one and `L` its length, so that the line reaches the next value on the last day of its stage. This is the construction of the crop coefficient curve of FAO-56 (Eq. 66, `i` the day of the season counted from one) as this unit reads it; the form is PROPOSED. The basal coefficient takes the three records of Table 17 (`initial`, `mid`, `end`). The root depth takes `initial_root_depth`, then `maximum_root_depth` at both the mid and the end values: it grows in a straight line over the development stage and stays at its maximum in the mid and late stages (D78, provisional; OPEN 27). Only the values the stage of the day needs are read: a day of the initial stage does not need `maximum_root_depth` nor the `mid` coefficient. The climatic adjustment of `Kcb` mid and end (FAO-56 Eq. 70, kept by D78) is not applied (OPEN 4).
18. **The surface layer of a fallow day takes D110 from records** (unit #217). D110 sets `fc = 0` and `fw = 1` on a fallow day. The two values are read as `cover_fraction` and `wetted_fraction` with the scope of a day with no crop, so that no value of the method is in the code; `cover_fraction_from_basal_coefficient`, its exponent and the upper limit are not used on that day. The wetted fraction of a day with a crop keeps its site record. The basal coefficient, `crop_height` and `curve_number` of a day with no crop are read as before, since D110 does not change them (OPEN 33, OPEN 35).
19. **A fallow is named by the crop sown after it** (`fallow.following_crop`). D110 gives one cover per fallow and D115 reads it from a table of the cover at sowing, by sequence, with no cap "before a winter cereal". The fallow of a day ends the day before the next sowing in the calendar, and the cover is read with the scope `{"crop": <that crop_id>, "management": "no_crop"}`. Every day of one fallow reads the same record, so the cover is constant over the fallow (D110, D115). A day with no sowing after it in the calendar is `MISSING_INPUT`; two crops sown on that date are `NOT_COMPUTABLE`. OPEN 31.
20. **The reduction of the total evaporable water is in proportion to the cover.** D110: "reduced by 5 % for each 10 % of soil surface effectively covered by an organic mulch" (FAO-56 chapter 11, "Surface covered with dead vegetation"). Form PROPOSED: `TEW_fallow = TEW × (1 − reduction × cover / increment)`, with the two constants as records (`mulch_total_evaporable_water_reduction`, `mulch_cover_increment`) and the cover a fraction of the surface; the readily evaporable water of the day is the smaller of its record and `TEW_fallow` (D110: "REW not above TEW"). A cover outside zero to one, an increment not above zero, a reduction below zero, or a reduction that leaves no evaporable water is `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, a `chain` item. OPEN 32.
21. **A depletion above the reduced total is not computed.** A day with a crop can end with the surface layer drier than the reduced total of the fallow that follows. The surface-layer balance holds the depletion within the total evaporable water, and no decision says how the depletion carried from the day before is brought within a smaller total, so that day is `NOT_COMPUTABLE` with a `chain` item naming OPEN 30, and the state stays missing after it. No rule is applied. OPEN 30.
22. **Weed-free fallow transpires nothing** (D111). On a day with no crop the balance is given no crop (`crop = None`), so it transpires nothing and its crop quantities are `NOT_APPLICABLE`; no weed or volunteer is modelled. This is the behaviour of the balance on a day with no crop since #165; unit #217 names it and tests it. OPEN 33.

## Numbers in the module

`fallow.py` holds `0` and `1`: the bounds of a fraction (the cover lies between zero and one; the increment lies above zero) and the whole of the total evaporable water in the form of choice 20. `exposure.py`, `_parameters.py` and `stages.py` hold one integer, `1`: days and months are counted from one (C5.1), the year before the start month, one day earlier, one year later, the last year of an inclusive range, and the one component or record a lookup expects. A test reads the four modules and fails on any other number and on any format specification in an f-string. Every number of the test file is a PLACEHOLDER chosen only so that every part accepts it and the chain gives a number every day. They are written with repeated digits (`2.22`, `0.555`), or as zero or one where a PROPOSED form needs the identity element; each zero is explained by a comment where it is written (for example, an albedo and a Stefan-Boltzmann constant of zero keep the net radiation above the soil heat flux, so the reference evapotranspiration stays above zero). None is a value of the method.
