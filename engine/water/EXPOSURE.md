# Water entry point

Unit `l1/water-ep3-parameters` (issue #153): entry point EP3 of `contracts/ENTRYPOINTS.md`, `compute_water_deficit_exposure`, in `engine/water/exposure.py`, exported by `engine/water/__init__.py`. It replaces the mechanical form of unit `l1/water-entrypoint` (issue #134). The lookup of a parameter lives in `engine/water/_parameters.py`.

Unit `l1/water-ep3-identifiers` (issue #191) makes EP3 read every parameter of the water records drafted for the lead developer's check (outside the repository; their ids, scopes and units only, never their values): the basal crop coefficient by crop and growth stage (FAO-56 Table 17, D78), the maximum root depth and the depletion fraction by crop (Tables 22, D78), the depth of the evaporation layer `Ze` and the two constants of the total evaporable water (D77), and the two bounds of PC-4 (D82) passed to `et0/` as `Et0Bounds`.

Unit `l1/water-crop-stages` (issue #206) gives EP3 the growth stages of each crop period. `engine/water/stages.py` places the four FAO-56 stages (initial, development, mid, late) end to end from the start of each calendar component (its sowing), using four stage lengths in days read from `parameters` by crop (`fao56_stage_length_<stage>`, PROPOSED). The basal crop coefficient of a crop day is then taken from its stage, with the straight line between the values of Table 17 that FAO-56 uses to build the crop coefficient curve (Eq. 66, form PROPOSED), and the root depth grows by stage from a new `initial_root_depth` to `maximum_root_depth` (D78, provisional curves, form PROPOSED). A crop period whose stage lengths are missing keeps the behaviour of D79: its days are missing with the reason, never guessed. Unit `l1/water-stage-tail-d135` (issue #224) applies [D135 to D137](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6887206691) of issue #77: stage lengths that do not fill the period are not stretched; they are cut at the harvest, or they leave a tail of days with no living crop up to the harvest, which EP3 runs as fallow days. EP2 does not change (D56). See choices 6, 16 and 17.

Unit `l1/water-fallow-residue` (issue #217) builds the fallow day of decisions D110, D111, D115 and D116 of #77. A fallow day is a day of the rotation, repeated over the record, on which no component carries a crop. On it, the fallow is weed-free (D111, declared as an assumption): no crop reaches the balance, so nothing is transpired. The surface layer takes the cover fraction `fc` and the wetted fraction `fw` from two records of a day with no crop (D110 gives `fc = 0`, `fw = 1`), and the total evaporable water is reduced by the surface residue cover of the fallow, "5 % for each 10 %" of the surface covered (D110, FAO-56 chapter 11, form PROPOSED), with the readily evaporable water not above it. The cover is one record per fallow, read by the crop sown after the fallow (D115: the cover at sowing), and it is the one record both engines read (D116). `engine/water/fallow.py` holds the three pure steps. Days with a crop are computed exactly as before. See choices 18 to 22 and OPEN 30 to 35.

Unit `l1/water-ep3-repeat-d47` (issue #253) lays the rotation over the record. EP3 reads the crop, the growth stage, the fallow and the tail of each day from the calendar repeated over the agricultural years of the record by `engine.calendar.repeat_over_record` (D47, D109 of #77), the helper EP1 and EP4 already use. A calendar whose own dates lie outside the record runs on the years of the record. A calendar the runner moves with `phase_calendar` (D132) runs with no change of the signature or the return. A component that starts before the record and ends inside it keeps its declared dates and is present from the first day of the record (D141). The rule of the repetition lives in `engine/calendar/repeat.py` only: this folder holds no copy of it. Nothing is laid out past the record: a fallow whose next sowing lies after the record has no following crop, and its days are missing with that reason (OPEN 41). See choices 5, 16 and 19 and OPEN 5, 31 and 39 to 42.

Unit `l1/water-indicator-d157` (issue #264) builds [D157](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6888807199) of #77: EP3 reports `water_deficit_exposure` as a number for an agricultural year whose daily balance was computed on every day. The value is the water transpired over the water demanded, each summed over the days of the year with a living crop (D105, D106, D136). Water transpired is the `transpiration` of the daily balance; water demanded is its `transpiration_demanded`, the basal crop coefficient times the reference evapotranspiration; neither is recomputed. The label "whole cycle, not critical period" (D105) is written verbatim at the start of the `source` of every value (D162), with no change of the results contract. A year with no day with a living crop is `NOT_APPLICABLE`; a year the chain could not compute keeps its own reason. Both time bases stay and no reason code is new (D163). See choices 15 and 23 and OPEN 16, 43 and 44.

Unit `l1/water-open30-d` builds [D185](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889974874) of issue #77, which closes OPEN 30: when the total evaporable water of a day (reduced on a fallow day by D110, which D186 confirms) is below the depletion the surface layer carries in, the depletion is kept and only the limit changes. The layer starts the day at its limit and evaporates nothing; the infiltration refills the part above the limit first; what is not refilled is carried on (`_layer_start` in `exposure.py`, `ChainDay.surface_depletion_carried`). No water is created or removed in the surface layer, and the column balance is unchanged in form. Before this unit such a day was `NOT_COMPUTABLE` and the rest of the record missing. See choice 21 and OPEN 30.

Unit `l1/water-decisions-d186-d217` (issue #333) brings this file and the texts of the code in line with [D186 to D191](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889974874) and [D214 to D217](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6892460770) of issue #77, and ends the `source` of every value with the clause of method facts of D215 (option S-b, `METHOD_FACTS` in `exposure.py`), each fact written only because the code guarantees it (choice 23). No other behaviour changes. See choices 16, 20 and 23 and OPEN 5, 14, 16, 29, 32, 39, 40, 43 and 44.

Later updates (D188 part 2 and `live_crop_by_day`): [D188](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889974874) part (2) (PR #403, unit `l1/calendar-lead-in-d188-part2`, points 6 and 13 of `engine/calendar/repeat.py`) lays out the cycle before the first whenever the first cycle starts after the first day of the record's years, so no day of the record's years lies before the described period (`described_from`). Unit `l1/live-crop-by-day` (PR #423, issue #399) moves the day-by-day crop rule (`repeat_calendar_over_record`, `component_of_day`, `LiveCropOverRecord`, and the texts including `CROP_UNKNOWN`) from `exposure.py` to `engine/water/living_crop.py` and exposes `live_crop_by_day`; EP3 builds its chain on `LiveCropOverRecord` with no change to any EP3 result, `exposure.py` keeps the text names as aliases, and `CROP_UNKNOWN` notes that since D188 part (2) (#403) no day of the record's years lies before `described_from`.

They take effect only when a person approves the pull request. Items under [OPEN](#open) need an answer from a person.

**What this unit does.** For every day of the climate record, with the crop of the day read from the rotation repeated over the record (D47), it chains the four internal parts of the water engine of D44: the reference evapotranspiration (`et0/`), the runoff by the Curve Number (`runoff/`), the evaporation coefficient `Ke` from the surface-layer balance (`evaporation/`) and the one-layer daily balance (`balance/`). Every constant and parameter of those parts is read from the argument `parameters` by its `id` and `scope`. The daily chain is reduced to one item per agricultural year of the record (D47).

**What it returns.** A number for an agricultural year whose daily balance was computed on every day and that has at least one day with a living crop: the ratio of D157, over the whole cycle of each crop and not over its critical period, which stays undefined (C1.4, D105). Every other year is missing with the reason that applies, never zero. With the catalogue of the repository a year is a number only when every record the chain reads has a value; a record that is absent leaves the year missing, naming it. The four parts are called, not changed. This folder holds no parameter value: not in the code, not in this file. The numbers of the test file are PLACEHOLDER, plainly not real.

## Signature

```text
compute_water_deficit_exposure(
    calendar: RotationCalendar,
    climate: SiteClimate,
    soil_slope: SiteSoilSlope,
    *,
    parameters: dict[str, dict],
) -> list[WaterDeficitExposure]
```

As EP3 is written in `contracts/ENTRYPOINTS.md` since #144 (Q3, D46, D56). `parameters` is passed by keyword only and has no default (G3). It is the catalogue exactly as `params.catalogue.load_folder` returns it for `params/records/`: every record, keyed by the path of its file. The models are imported only as `contracts.inputs.<module>` and `contracts.results.models`; `Missing` is the one of `contracts/results/models.py`; no new missing shape is defined.

The entry point reads a record only through the pure readers of `params.catalogue`: `identity` to find it by `id` and `scope`, and `read_value` to read it (G1). It never calls `load_folder` and reads no file. `read_range` is not used: no range is computed, because one call is one scenario and no indicator value is a number (Q6).

`soil_slope` is checked (G3, G5) and not read: no part reads a soil property yet. The soil's available water of D49 is read as the parameter `column_capacity` when a record exists (OPEN 7).

## What it returns

Always a list with at least one item. Every item keeps `indicator`, `direction` and `unit` (C3.1), read from `contracts/results/models.py`. A missing item carries no provenance, no source, no range and no `range_missing` (the model forbids them). A number carries what the model asks of a value (choice 23): the provenance label `SIMULATED`, a `source` text that starts with the label "whole cycle, not critical period" (D105, D162) and says what the value is computed from, no assumption mark, no range, and `range_missing` with reason `NOT_AVAILABLE`. A value lies between zero and one: the balance never transpires more than is demanded.

| Case | What is returned |
|---|---|
| `calendar.ag_year_start.month` is missing | One item, `time_basis: "rotation"`, reason `MISSING_INPUT`, detail names the start month |
| `climate.daily_series` has no `precipitation` series | One item, `time_basis: "rotation"`, reason `MISSING_INPUT`, detail names `precipitation` |
| Otherwise | One item per agricultural year of the `precipitation` series (D47), `time_basis: "ag_year"`, with its `ag_year`, each missing with the first reason of the table below, or a number (D157) |

Reason of each yearly item, the first row that applies:

| Situation | Reason | Convention |
|---|---|---|
| The calendar is infeasible (`feasibility.feasible` is false) | `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, every year; the chain does not run | G4, first row |
| The daily series the water engine reads that are present do not share one time zone | `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, every year; the chain does not run | C6.3; G4, sixth row |
| A series the water engine reads that is present does not cover the year from its first day to its last | `NO_COVERAGE`; detail names each such series | C5.5 |
| A series, or the latitude or elevation of the point, arrives in a unit string the parts do not accept | `MISSING_INPUT` (PROPOSED, Q11); detail names each one and both unit strings; the chain does not run | C1.1; G4, seventh row |
| The daily chain has no value on a day of the year (the storage at the end of a balance day is missing) | The reason of the first such day: `MISSING_INPUT`, or `NOT_COMPUTABLE` when a part refused a value; detail below | G4, second to fourth rows and the two rows of a parameter; C3.3 |
| The daily balance was computed on every day of the year, and no day of the year has a living crop | `NOT_APPLICABLE`; detail names D106, D136 and D157 | D157 |
| The daily balance was computed on every day of the year, and the water demanded, summed over its days with a living crop, is not above zero | `NOT_COMPUTABLE` (D214): the ratio has no denominator | G4 |
| The daily balance was computed on every day of the year (otherwise) | **A number**: the sum of `transpiration` over the sum of `transpiration_demanded` of the daily balance, over the days of the year with a living crop | D157, D105, D106, D136, D47 |

The daily series the water engine reads are `precipitation` (D23) and the five inputs of the reference evapotranspiration, read from `CLIMATE_QUANTITIES` of `engine/water/et0/quantities.py`. The unit string of each is the one the parts transcribed from `contracts/CLIMATE_QUANTITIES.md` (`PRECIPITATION_UNIT` of `runoff/`, `ACCEPTED_UNITS` of `et0/`). The point is accepted in the unit strings D55 sets for the climate adapter: `degree` for latitude, `m` for elevation.

### The daily chain

The chain runs from the first day that every series it reads covers to the last day of the `precipitation` series, one day after the other, never resetting a state (D44). For each day:

1. **Reference evapotranspiration**: `reference_evapotranspiration_from_climate_with_bounds` of `et0/`, with `Et0Constants` and the two bounds of D82 (`Et0Bounds`, PC-4) built from the catalogue, `PointUnits` of D55, and a unit-guard code that is never emitted (units are checked before the chain runs).
2. **Crop of the day** (PROPOSED, choice 5): the calendar is repeated over the agricultural years of the record by `engine.calendar.repeat_over_record` (called through `living_crop.repeat_calendar_over_record`, #423), on the dates of the `precipitation` series (D47, D109). The crop of the day is read through `living_crop.component_of_day` (`engine/water/living_crop.py`, #423): the crop of the one component of the repeated rotation whose dates cover the day, in whichever cycle, with the cycle before the first laid out whenever the first cycle starts after the first day of the record's years (D141; D188 part 2, #403, points 6 and 13 of `engine/calendar/repeat.py`); no crop when no component can cover the day (including days before the first cycle that no component covers); `living_crop.component_of_day` keeps the `day < repeated.described_from` guard returning `MISSING_INPUT` (`CROP_UNKNOWN`), which since D188 part 2 (#403, #423) does not apply to any day of the record's years (OPEN 5); not known (`MISSING_INPUT`, naming the component and the reason the calendar gives for the date) when a component with a missing date could cover the day within its cycle and no dated component covers it; `NOT_COMPUTABLE` when two components cover the day. On a day with a crop, the three records of `basal_crop_coefficient` of the crop (one per stage) and every parameter read by crop (the table below: `maximum_root_depth`, `readily_available_fraction`, `initial_root_depth` and the four stage lengths) are read, so that a missing record is named. The basal coefficient and the root depth of the day come from `_growth_of_day` (choice 6): the stages of the component are placed from its start in its cycle (`stages.place_stages`, choice 16), and each value is taken from the stage of the day (`stages.by_stage`, choice 17). The crop of the balance is built from them and `readily_available_fraction`. A stage length with no value makes the basal coefficient and the crop of the balance `MISSING_INPUT`, with a `chain` item naming the unknown stage and D79; stages that cannot be placed make them `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, with a `chain` item saying why; a record the stage of the day needs that is missing makes them `MISSING_INPUT`, naming it. The states carried from day to day stay missing after such a day.
   On a day with no crop (unit #217), the crop of the day is `None`: the basal coefficient, `crop_height` and `curve_number` are read with the scope of a day with no crop, no crop reaches the balance (D111), and the surface layer of the fallow is built as in step 4.
3. **Runoff**: `runoff_day` of `runoff/`, with the precipitation of the day, the curve number of the crop of the day (or of a day with no crop) and `CurveNumberConstants` from the catalogue.
4. **`Ke`**: the total evaporable water, computed once on the first day of the chain from the layer (`total_evaporable_water` of `evaporation/`, choice 8); the climate term of the upper limit (`climate_adjusted_upper_limit`, from the wind speed of the day, the minimum relative humidity and the crop height), the upper limit (`upper_limit_of_crop_coefficient`), the cover fraction (`cover_fraction_from_basal_coefficient`), then `step` of `evaporation/`, carrying the depletion of the surface layer from day to day. On a day with no crop (D110, unit #217) the upper limit and the cover fraction are not computed: `step` is given the cover fraction and the wetted fraction of the records of a day with no crop, and a total evaporable water reduced by the residue cover of the fallow (`fallow.mulched_total_evaporable_water`, read by the crop sown after the fallow in the repeated rotation, `fallow.following_crop`, choice 19), with the readily evaporable water not above it (`fallow.readily_evaporable_water_not_above`). A depletion carried into a day above its total (the reduced total of a fallow day) is kept: the step starts at the total, the infiltration refills the part above it first, and what is not refilled is carried on (choice 21, D185).
5. **Balance**: `step` of `balance/`, with the precipitation, the runoff, the reference evapotranspiration, the `Ke` of the surface layer and the crop of the day (`CropDay` from the catalogue, `None` on a day with no crop), carrying the storage from day to day.

The entry point calls the daily `step` of the surface layer and of the balance, not `run_surface_layer` and `run_balance`, so that a value one part refuses on one day makes that day missing instead of raising for the whole record. Once the state at the end of a day is missing, every later day starts from a missing state that names the first cause, exactly as the two `run_` functions carry it.

### Format of `detail` (PROPOSED)

English, for developers. A year the chain did not compute reads `water_deficit_exposure is not computed for agricultural year <Y>: the daily water balance has no value on <n> day(s) of the year, the first on <date> (<code>: <detail of that day>); <item>; <item>; ...`. The items, in this order:

| Item | When |
|---|---|
| `input MISSING_INPUT: the climate record has no daily series '<name>' (G4, third row)` | One per absent series |
| `input MISSING_INPUT: the daily series '<name>' holds <n> day(s) without a value in agricultural year <Y>, the first on <date>, and no gap is filled (G4, fourth row, C3.4)` | One per series with days held missing in the year |
| `input <code>: the <latitude or elevation> of the point of the climate record is missing: <its detail>` | One per missing value of the point the reference evapotranspiration reads; longitude is not read |
| `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameters with no record in parameters (D56), scope <scope>: '<id>', '<id>', ...` | One per scope, naming every parameter with no record that was first needed on or before the last day of the year |
| `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameter '<id>' with scope <scope>: the record holds no value: <reason of the record>` | One per record whose value is `null` |
| `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameter '<id>' with scope <scope>: the record is not read: <why>` | One per record that `read_value` refuses (its message), or per two records that share `id` and `scope` |
| `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameters that cannot be looked up: the record(s) '<key>', ... have no well-formed 'id' and 'scope', so they cannot be compared and could be records of this parameter (F3c of params/README.md); ...`, then `parameter MISSING_INPUT: parameters that cannot be looked up, scope <scope>: '<id>', '<id>', ...` | When a record of `parameters` has no well-formed `id` and `scope`: the malformed records named once by their key, then the parameters grouped by scope, as above |
| `chain <code>: <detail>` | One per problem of the chain met on or before the last day of the year: a crop that is not known (a day before the described period `described_from` with `CROP_UNKNOWN` in `engine/water/living_crop.py` (#423), which since D188 part 2 (#403) does not occur on any day of the record's years, OPEN 5; a component with a missing date, named with the reason of the calendar), two components on one day, a value a part refuses (`<part> refuses a value: <its message>`), a fallow with no crop after it in the rotation laid out over the record, or whose following crop is not known (OPEN 31; OPEN 41, kept missing by D187), a reduction of the total evaporable water the method does not compute |
| `indicator: the definition of the indicator is still incomplete (C1.4): the critical period of each crop is not defined, so the ratio ... is taken over the whole cycle of each crop (decisions D105 and D157 of issue #77), and the state of the balance that the descriptive measures use is not defined` | Always, last: what the definition still leaves open (the constant `DEFINITION_INCOMPLETE`, whose name is kept because `validation/test_chain_examples.py` reads it) |

A scope is written as JSON text with sorted keys: `{}`, `{"crop": "<crop identifier>"}`, `{"management": "no_crop"}`, `{"crop": "<crop identifier>", "management": "no_crop"}`. The other details are one fixed text each: infeasible calendar, time zones, start month, `precipitation` absent, a year not covered (`the daily series '<name>', ... do not cover agricultural year <Y> from its first day to its last (C5.5)`), units not accepted (naming Q11 and each unit string), a computed year with no living crop (`water_deficit_exposure does not apply to agricultural year <Y>: the daily water balance was computed on every day of the year, and no day of the year has a living crop ...`) and a computed year in which nothing was demanded (`... summed over its <n> day(s) with a living crop, is not above zero ...`).

### The value and its `source` (D215, D216)

A value is `water transpired / water demanded`, with `water transpired` the sum of `DayResult.transpiration` and `water demanded` the sum of `DayResult.transpiration_demanded` of `balance/`, over the days of the agricultural year on which the balance was given a crop (`_computed` in `exposure.py`). The sums are `math.fsum`. Its `source` reads `whole cycle, not critical period (decisions D105 and D162 of issue #77); simulated by engine.water.compute_water_deficit_exposure as decision D157 states it: the transpiration of the daily water balance over its demanded transpiration (the basal crop coefficient times the reference evapotranspiration), each summed over the <n> day(s) with a living crop of agricultural year <Y>, the first on <date> and the last on <date>; days with no crop and the tail after maturity are left out (D106, D136); the daily balance runs on the daily series of the climate record, on the rotation calendar repeated over the record (D47) and on the parameter records listed in engine/water/EXPOSURE.md; method facts of this engine: ...`, the clause of method facts of D215 (`METHOD_FACTS`, choice 23 b). Its `range_missing` reads `no uncertainty range is computed: one call is one scenario, and how a range passes through the daily water balance is not built (C2.3, Q6 of contracts/ENTRYPOINTS.md, decision D216 of issue #77)`.

## Parameters

PROPOSED: one identifier and one scope for each parameter the chain reads. Where a part's README table proposes an identifier it is used as it is; the identifiers of `et0/` and of the climate term of `evaporation/` are written there as `<group>.<field>`, and a record `id` holds no dot (`params/README.md`), so the dot is written as an underscore. The value of every row is a person's (D34); 100 signed water records are now in `params/records/water/` (45 in #262, 43 for site, `soybean` and `no_crop` in #417, and 12 for `wheat` and `maize` in #446, `46efd05`), while 10 `wheat` and `maize` records still await signature (#433).

The scope column says how a parameter is looked up (PROPOSED; the words allowed under each scope key are question 4 of `params/README.md`, OPEN):

- `site`: the empty scope `{}`. One record for the field and the method.
- `crop or no crop`: `{"crop": "<crop_id>"}`, the `crop_id` of the calendar component of the day; on a day with no crop, `{"management": "no_crop"}`.
- `crop and stage, or no crop`: on a day with a crop, `{"crop": "<crop_id>", "stage": "<stage>"}` for each of the stage words `initial`, `mid` and `end` of the drafted records (PROPOSED; the words allowed under `stage` are question 4 of `params/README.md`); on a day with no crop, `{"management": "no_crop"}`.
- `crop`: `{"crop": "<crop_id>"}` only; read on a day with a crop.
- `no crop`: `{"management": "no_crop"}` only; read on a day with no crop (unit #217).
- `site, on a day with no crop`: the empty scope `{}`, read on the first day with no crop that needs it (unit #217).
- `no crop, by the crop that follows`: `{"crop": "<crop_id>", "management": "no_crop"}`, with the `crop_id` of the component sown first after the day; read on a day with no crop (unit #217, choice 19).

The lookup is exact: a record with the empty scope is never read in place of one that names a crop, nor the other way round (question 5 of `params/README.md`, OPEN).

## PROPOSED choices

Where EP3, the brief, the conventions and the decisions do not settle something, the option that invents least. Each is a proposal; the question is under [OPEN](#open).

1. **Time basis.** Yearly items are on `ag_year` (D47); the two cases where the years cannot be laid out give one item on `rotation`, as in #134. OPEN 1, OPEN 2.
2. **Precedence**: infeasible calendar, time zones, coverage, units, then the chain. OPEN 12.
3. **Scope words and exact lookup**, as above. OPEN 6.
4. **New identifiers**: `minimum_relative_humidity`, `column_capacity`, `column_depth`, `initial_storage`, `crop_height`, `curve_number`, `basal_crop_coefficient`, `readily_available_fraction`, `field_capacity_water_content`, `wilting_point_water_content`. Where a part has an argument of that name, the name is kept; `capacity` is written `column_capacity` so that it is not ambiguous in a catalogue shared with other engines. OPEN 6.
5. **Crop of a day from the rotation repeated over the record** (unit #253; D47, D109, D132, D141), as in step 2 of the chain. Originally written in `exposure.py` as `_over_record` and `_component_of_day`, the repetition wrapper (`repeat_calendar_over_record`), day-by-day lookup (`component_of_day`), `LiveCropOverRecord`, and `live_crop_by_day` now live in `engine/water/living_crop.py` (#423, issue #399), and EP3 builds its chain on `LiveCropOverRecord` with no change to any EP3 result. `repeat_calendar_over_record` calls `engine.calendar.repeat_over_record` with the calendar and the first and last day of the `precipitation` series, the series that marks the record for EP1 too, and `component_of_day` reads the result as EP1 reads it (`_covering` of `engine/erosion/soil_loss.py`). Nothing of the repetition is computed in this folder: the cycle length, the shift of the dates, the lead-in, the cut at the end of the record and a 29 February in a year without one are the helper's (its points 1 to 13). Since D188 part 2 (#403, points 6 and 13 of `engine/calendar/repeat.py`), the lead-in cycle before the first is laid out whenever the first cycle starts after the first day of the record's years, so days before the first cycle are days with no crop (unless covered by a lead-in or undated component) rather than `CROP_UNKNOWN`. A calendar moved by `phase_calendar` is repeated like any other, so each phase of D109 is one call with its own calendar and lists every agricultural year of the record. **A crossing component (D141)** arrives as a component of the cycle before the first, with its declared dates: it is the crop of every day from the first day of the record to its end, its stages are counted from its declared sowing (choice 16), and the states at the start of the chain are the records of the site, as for any first day (OPEN 39). Where `phase_calendar` cannot place a crossing component it gives it missing dates with their reason; the days it could cover then have no known crop, naming it and that reason, and nothing is guessed (OPEN 40). The entry point lists the years itself and raises `ValueError`, as EP1 does, if the helper lays out other years from the same record and start month. OPEN 5.
6. **A day with a crop takes its basal coefficient and root depth from its growth stage** (unit #206; it replaces the choice of unit #191, under which every crop day was missing). D78 gives the basal coefficient one value per growth stage (FAO-56 Table 17) and the root depth a crop-specific growth (FAO-56 Annex 8, provisional), and both need the stages. The calendar carries no growth stage and EP2 does not change (D56), so EP3 places them itself, per component, from the stage lengths of its crop (choice 16), and takes both values of each day from its stage (choice 17), in one function, `_growth_of_day`. On a day with a crop EP3 reads the three stage records of `basal_crop_coefficient` and every parameter by crop (a missing one is named). When a stage length has no value, D79 (USER-U) applies: missing stages stay missing for the water indicator they affect, so every day of that period is `MISSING_INPUT` naming the unknown stage and D79, and, as the surface layer and the balance carry their state, so is every later day. `crop_height`, `cover_fraction_exponent` and `curve_number` stay one value per crop (not in the original drafts; signed for `soybean` and `no_crop` in #417, and for `wheat` and `maize` in #446 along with `initial_root_depth`, `maximum_root_depth` and `readily_available_fraction`; the four `fao56_stage_length_*` records for `wheat` and `maize` and `surface_residue_cover` for fallow before `wheat` and `maize`—10 records in all—still await signature in #433). OPEN 4.
7. **The minimum relative humidity is a parameter of the site.** The climate term of the upper limit needs it every day, and no daily series carries it: humidity travels as dew point (D25). OPEN 3.
8. **`total_evaporable_water` is computed from the surface layer** (unit `l1/water-ep3-identifiers`, issue #191): `total_evaporable_water` of `evaporation/` with the depth of the layer `Ze` (D77) and the two constants of its form, read from the catalogue, and the water contents at field capacity and wilting point of the layer, read as two parameters of the site because no soil property reaches the engine with an agreed identifier. It is computed once, on the first day of the chain. A result the part does not compute (`NOT_COMPUTABLE`), or an operand it refuses, is a `chain` item; a missing operand is named as a parameter. OPEN 13.
9. **The cover fraction of a day with a crop is computed from `Kcb`** (`cover_fraction_from_basal_coefficient`), not observed: no observed cover fraction reaches the engine. On a day with no crop it is the record of D110 (choice 18). OPEN 4.
10. **`Ke` reaches the balance as the surface layer gives it**, also on a day the surface layer runs short (OPEN 2 of `evaporation/README.md`), except on a day with no crop that starts at its total, where D185 applies (choice 21). OPEN 11.
11. **Units are checked before the chain runs.** A series or a value of the point in another unit string makes every covered year `MISSING_INPUT`, naming it and Q11; nothing is converted. Because of that check, the unit-guard code passed to `et0/` and `runoff/` (`MISSING_INPUT`) is never emitted. The unit strings of the parameter records are not checked. OPEN 8, OPEN 9.
12. **A value a part refuses is `NOT_COMPUTABLE`.** The parts raise `ValueError` for a number outside what they accept (a negative depth, a storage above the capacity, a readily evaporable water above the total). That number comes from the catalogue or the climate record, not from a defect of the caller of EP3, so G5 does not let EP3 raise: the day is missing with `NOT_COMPUTABLE` (G4, "the method does not cover the case"), naming the part and its message, and later days start from a missing state. OPEN 10.
13. **The days of the chain** run from the first day every series it reads covers, to the last day of the `precipitation` series; `initial_storage` and `initial_depletion` are the states at the start of that day. A year before that day is `NO_COVERAGE` anyway, since a series does not cover its first day. OPEN 14.
14. **A record `read_value` refuses**, or two records with one `id` and `scope`, give `MISSING_INPUT` naming the parameter and why: the value the computation needs is not available to it (G4, second row). The runner passes only a catalogue that `check_catalogue` passed (G3), so this happens only if it does not. A record anywhere in `parameters` without a well-formed `id` and `scope` cannot be compared, so it could be a record of any parameter: it is reported, not skipped (F3c of `params/README.md`), and **every** parameter is `MISSING_INPUT`, naming it and the key of each such record, as EP1 does in `engine/erosion/_parameters.py` (#158).
15. **What a year needs, and which of its days count** (D157). A year is computed when the storage at the end of every one of its balance days is a number. Every day stays in the daily balance, which never resets (D44). **A day with a living crop** is a day on which the balance was given a crop: its `transpiration_demanded` is a number. On a fallow day and on a day of the tail after maturity the balance is given no crop and answers `NOT_APPLICABLE` for the crop quantities; those days do not enter the ratio (D106, D136). The ratio of a year is summed over its days with a living crop, whichever cycle they belong to, so a cycle that crosses the start of the agricultural year gives its days before that start to one year and its days from it to the next (D157: "split by day"). A year with no such day is `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
16. **The stages of a crop period** (units #206 and #224, `stages.place_stages`; [D135 to D137](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6887206691) of issue #77). The four stages follow one another from the start date of the component, its sowing: day one of the period is day one of the initial stage, and each stage begins the day after the one before it ends. Each length is read by crop (scope `{"crop": <crop_id>}`), once per component and cycle of the repeated rotation, on the first day of it the chain meets. That day is not always the sowing day: for a component sown before the record and present on its first day (D141), it is the first day of the record, and the stage of that day is still counted from the declared sowing. The difference matters only for a record with validity dates, which would be read on that day rather than on the sowing day. A length must be a whole number of days of at least one; otherwise the whole period is `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, with a `chain` item naming the component and why, and no stage is guessed. **The lengths are not stretched to the period (D135).** Stages that run past the declared harvest (D72) are cut at the harvest: the stage in which the harvest falls ends on it and the stages after it are not placed. A cut stage keeps its catalogue length, so its straight line (choice 17) is the one of the whole stage, ended early, and does not reach the next value (FAO-56 chapter 6, early harvest, as D135 reads it; PROPOSED, OPEN 38). Stages that end before the harvest leave a **tail**: the days from the day after the end of the late stage to the harvest (`PlacedStages.tail`). **A tail day is a day with no living crop (D136)**: EP3 runs it as a fallow day (choices 18 to 22, unit #217, unchanged), with the records of a day with no crop and the residue cover of the fallow that follows (the crop sown next, choice 19). No crop reaches the balance, so its potential transpiration is zero and it stays out of the ratio (D106); it stays in the daily balance, which never resets (D44), and counts towards the water at the next sowing (D50). Days are calendar days, so a 29 February inside the period only lengthens the tail or shortens the cut by one day. The lengths are FAO-56 Table 11 as a transferred reference, labelled not local and provisional until a traceable local phenology exists (D137, extending D78 and D79); a phenology replaces the records, not the code. The stage lengths depend on the crop only, not on the sowing date (OPEN 26). **Label line**: replaced by the `source` of [D215](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6892460770) (choice 23): the label of D105 first and verbatim (D162), what the value is computed from and over which days, then the clause of method facts, which names the stage lengths as read by crop from the parameter catalogue (D137) without saying which table the catalogue holds. OPEN 36 to OPEN 38.
17. **The value of a day by stage** (unit #206, `stages.by_stage`): constant in the initial and mid stages, and a straight line in the development and late stages, `start + (j / L) × (next − start)`, with `j` the day of the stage counted from one and `L` its length, so that the line reaches the next value on the last day of its stage. This is the construction of the crop coefficient curve of FAO-56 (Eq. 66, `i` the day of the season counted from one) as this unit reads it; the form is PROPOSED. The basal coefficient takes the three records of Table 17 (`initial`, `mid`, `end`). The root depth takes `initial_root_depth`, then `maximum_root_depth` at both the mid and the end values: it grows in a straight line over the development stage and stays at its maximum in the mid and late stages (D78, provisional; OPEN 27). Only the values the stage of the day needs are read: a day of the initial stage does not need `maximum_root_depth` nor the `mid` coefficient. The climatic adjustment of `Kcb` mid and end (FAO-56 Eq. 70, kept by D78) is not applied (OPEN 4).
18. **The surface layer of a fallow day takes D110 from records** (unit #217). D110 sets `fc = 0` and `fw = 1` on a fallow day. The two values are read as `cover_fraction` and `wetted_fraction` with the scope of a day with no crop, so that no value of the method is in the code; `cover_fraction_from_basal_coefficient`, its exponent and the upper limit are not used on that day. The wetted fraction of a day with a crop keeps its site record. The basal coefficient, `crop_height` and `curve_number` of a day with no crop are read as before, since D110 does not change them (OPEN 33, OPEN 35). The records `cover_fraction` and `wetted_fraction` with the scope of a day with no crop must hold the values D110 fixes; EP3 reads them as given and does not check them against D110 (remark of the verification of #226).
19. **A fallow is named by the crop sown after it** (`fallow.following_crop`). D110 gives one cover per fallow and D115 reads it from a table of the cover at sowing, by sequence, with no cap "before a winter cereal". The fallow of a day ends the day before the next sowing in the rotation repeated over the record (unit #253), and the cover is read with the scope `{"crop": <that crop_id>, "management": "no_crop"}`. Every day of one fallow reads the same record, so the cover is constant over the fallow (D110, D115). The fallow after the last component of a cycle is followed by the first sowing of the next cycle, when the helper lays that sowing out. **Only the components `repeat_over_record` lays out are read**, and it is called once, with the record as it is: D109 cuts the last cycle at the end of the record, and the helper lays out no component that starts after the last day of the record's years. So the fallow after the last sowing laid out, whose next sowing lies after the record, has no crop that follows it: its days are `MISSING_INPUT`, as a calendar with no sowing after its last component was before this unit, and no sowing past the record is placed or guessed (OPEN 41). A component with a start date is a sowing, with or without an end date. A component without a start date that could be sown after the day and on or before the next dated sowing leaves the following crop not known (`MISSING_INPUT`, naming it); two crops sown on that date are `NOT_COMPUTABLE`. OPEN 31, OPEN 41.
20. **The reduction of the total evaporable water is in proportion to the cover.** D110, confirmed by [D186](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889974874): the total evaporable water, after FAO-56 chapter 11, "Surface covered with dead vegetation", "Dual crop coefficient": "reducing the value for TEW by 5% for each 10% of soil surface that is effectively covered by an organic mulch". The form, proportional to the cover, is declared as a reading by D186 (FAO-56 says neither proportional nor by whole steps of 10 %; the covers of D115 are multiples of 10 %, where both readings agree): `TEW_fallow = TEW × (1 − reduction × cover / increment)`, with the two constants as records (`mulch_total_evaporable_water_reduction`, `mulch_cover_increment`) and the cover a fraction of the surface; the readily evaporable water of the day is the smaller of its record and `TEW_fallow` (D110: "REW not above TEW"). A cover outside zero to one, an increment not above zero, a reduction below zero, or a reduction that leaves no evaporable water is `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, a `chain` item. OPEN 32, its first part closed by D186.
21. **A depletion above the total of the day is kept; only the limit changes** ([D185](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889974874) of issue #77, built by unit `l1/water-open30-d`). A day with a crop can end with the surface layer drier than the reduced total of the fallow that follows, and the same holds on the first day of a tail after maturity (D136). The depletion is the evaporation accumulated since the layer was last wetted (FAO-56 chapter 7, Eq. 77), and the total is the limit at which that evaporation stops (Eq. 78); D110, which D186 confirms, lowers the limit, not the water the layer has lost. So the depletion carried in is kept (`_layer_start`): the step of `evaporation/` starts the day at the total, its limit, and the layer evaporates nothing; the infiltration of the day (the precipitation less the runoff the step applies) refills the part above the total first, and only what is left of it is given to the step as precipitation; the part not refilled is carried to the next day on top of the depletion at the end of the day (`ChainDay.surface_depletion_carried`). Evaporation resumes when the depletion falls below the total (D185): with the readily evaporable water below the total, from the day after the rain that refills the part above it, since `Kr` follows the depletion at the start of the day; with the readily evaporable water at the total, which D110 allows, on that same day, through the stage-1 rule of the step. On a day with no crop that starts at its total, the column is given the evaporation the layer supplied (`_column_evaporation_coefficient`): none while the layer stays at its limit, whatever the readily evaporable water, although with the readily evaporable water at the total the stage-1 rule of the step gives `Kr` of one there. No water is created or removed in the surface layer: across days, the change of the carried depletion is `−I + DP + E/few` with the whole infiltration of the day, and the column balance (D44) is given the whole precipitation and closes as before. When the total rises again at the next sowing, the depletion is kept as it is. The same rule holds on any day whose total is below the depletion carried in, for a depletion within the total of the layer before any residue (the one of a day with a crop): the chain never carries more than that, so a record `initial_depletion` above it is still passed to the step and refused (`NOT_COMPUTABLE`, choice 12). Options not taken, as D185 lists them: (a) leave the day missing, as before this unit, which leaves the rest of the record missing from the first fallow with a conflict; (b) bring the depletion down to the reduced total, which creates that excess as evaporable water once per fallow and breaks the balance of the layer on that day; (c) scale the depletion by the ratio of the totals, which also changes the state when there is no conflict. OPEN 30, closed by D185.
22. **Weed-free fallow transpires nothing** (D111). On a day with no crop the balance is given no crop (`crop = None`), so it transpires nothing and its crop quantities are `NOT_APPLICABLE`; no weed or volunteer is modelled. This is the behaviour of the balance on a day with no crop since #165; unit #217 names it and tests it. OPEN 33.
23. **What a value carries** (unit #264; decided by [D214 to D217](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6892460770) of issue #77, unit #333). ## Numbers in the module

`fallow.py` holds `0` and `1`: the bounds of a fraction (the cover lies between zero and one; the increment lies above zero) and the whole of the total evaporable water in the form of choice 20. `exposure.py`, `_parameters.py` and `stages.py` hold the integer `1`: days and months are counted from one (C5.1), the year before the start month, one day earlier, one year later, the last year of an inclusive range, and the one component or record a lookup expects. `exposure.py` also holds `0` since unit #264: the demanded sum of a year must lie above zero for the ratio to exist (choice 23). A test reads the four modules and fails on any other number and on any format specification in an f-string. Every number of the test file is a PLACEHOLDER chosen only so that every part accepts it and the chain gives a number every day. They are written with repeated digits (`2.22`, `0.555`), or as zero or one where a PROPOSED form needs the identity element; each zero is explained by a comment where it is written (for example, an albedo and a Stefan-Boltzmann constant of zero keep the net radiation above the soil heat flux, so the reference evapotranspiration stays above zero). None is a value of the method.
