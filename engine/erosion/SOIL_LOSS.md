# Soil-loss entry point

Entry point EP1 of `contracts/ENTRYPOINTS.md`, `compute_soil_loss`, in `engine/erosion/soil_loss.py`, exported by `engine/erosion/__init__.py`. The lookup of a parameter is in the private module `engine/erosion/_parameters.py`.

- Unit `l0/erosion-entrypoint` (issue #115, merged as #125): the mechanical form. It lays out the agricultural years of the climate record and their months.
- Unit `l0/erosion-ep1-parameters` (issue #154, merged as #158): the argument `parameters` (D46, D56), and the factors the code then built could compute from it.
- Unit `l0/erosion-ep1-c-r` (issue #173, PR #187): `R` and `C` wired to `engine/erosion/r_factor/` and `engine/erosion/c_factor/`, every constant read from `parameters`. Revised after the review of PR #187 to follow decisions recorded on `main` after the brief: the route of the first release (D69, D84, USER-G) and the forms of PC-1.
- Unit `l1/calendar-repeat-d47` (issue #207): the wiring of the rotation repeated over the years of the record (D47), built in `engine/calendar/repeat.py`. The daily-index route reads the crop and the last harvest of each day from that repetition, so a calendar dated after the record (as every real calendar is) gives a number for every covered year.
- Unit `l0/erosion-c-main-route` (issue #218): the main route computes `C` for each agricultural year through `engine/erosion/c_factor/` with the design of decision D129 of issue #77, weighted by the twelve climatological monthly fractions (D84, D101), over the rotation repeated over the years of the record (D47). When a record of that `C` is missing, `C` falls back to the declared `rusle_c_factor` (M1.2, D125), a PROPOSED rule the lead developer asked for, and the source says so. See [C of the agricultural year](#c-of-the-agricultural-year-d129).

> **Status: PROPOSED** (blueprint section 3.7). The parameter identifiers and their scope, the monthly value of the main route, the crop and the residue of a day, the series by days after planting and how it is read between and beyond its days, the residue mass at harvest from a ratio and a yield, the conversion record and its unit string, the fallback to the declared `C`, the soil-loss ratio of a month, `C` by agricultural year, the reading of a unit string, the order of the checks, the reason code of each value, the format of every `detail`, the source text of a computed value and the test cases below were written by builder sessions. They take effect only when a person approves the pull request. Items under [OPEN](#open) need an answer from a person.

**This unit holds no parameter value.** No coefficient, no default and no stand-in number: not in the code, not in a test, not in this file. `params/records/`, the catalogue the service loads, holds 120 checked or declared records on `main` (`9f6c2a7`; #428): 26 erosion records at its top (#203), 88 water records in `params/records/water/` (#262, #417), and six residue records in `params/records/residues/`. The 67 records of the computed `C` listed under [Parameters EP1 reads](#parameters-ep1-reads) (unit l0/c-records, checked or declared by the lead developer under D34; D129, D131, [D142 to D145](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6887830990)) for the crops `soybean`, `wheat` and `maize` are in two places (D148, D158 and D164, next paragraph): 61 in `params/withdrawn/computed_c/`, outside that catalogue, and six in `params/records/residues/`, inside it, because the residue ledger reads them too. A catalogue that holds all 67, with a calendar whose crops carry those words, gets the computed `C`; a catalogue that lacks some of them, or a calendar with other crop words (the PLACEHOLDER examples), finds no record and takes the fallback, the declared product. By [D145](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6887830990) the demonstration was to show the computed `C`, knowing that for soybean with winter fallow it is about 0.53 against the 0.036 measured (D125).

**[D148](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6888128333) amends D145 and D146: the computed `C` is withdrawn from the demonstration.** Its per-rotation values are not shown, neither as absolute values nor as a comparison. Its code (#229) and its records (#237) stay in the repository as a documented capability that is not validated. Decision [D158](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889049425) of issue #77 (lead developer, 15 November 2026) says where the withdrawal takes place: in the catalogue the service loads. Until then the 67 records lay under `params/records/computed_c/`, the service loaded them with the rest of `params/records/`, and EP1 computed `C` from them for both rotations of D128. [D164](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889521276) amends D158: six of the 67, the residue/yield ratio and the yield of each crop (`rusle2_residue_yield_ratio`, `rusle2_crop_yield`), are also read by the residue ledger (EP4) and stay in the catalogue; the other 61 leave. Unit l0/computed-c-out-of-catalogue moved the 61, unchanged, to `params/withdrawn/computed_c/`, which the service does not load (`params/README.md`, "Files in `withdrawn/`"), and the six, unchanged, to `params/records/residues/`. EP1 does not compute `C` from the six alone: records of the computed `C` are missing, so it takes the fallback. The headline soil loss is the declared-`C` figure of D125 (the value the fallback of the main route gives): with the service's catalogue EP1 now returns it for every rotation, every phase and every covered year, and the source of each value says declared `C`. The check of EP1 against the soil loss measured on the La Estanzuela runoff plots, whose result D148 records as not passed for the computed `C`, is the entry [EP1: plausibility against the La Estanzuela runoff plots [E7]](../../docs/VALIDATION.md#ep1-plausibility-against-the-la-estanzuela-runoff-plots-20172019-e7) of `docs/VALIDATION.md`. This note changes no code. Unit l0/computed-c-out-of-catalogue changes no code of EP1 either: it moves records and updates tests and texts.

## Two routes

| Route | Function | Called by the runner | `R` | `C` | Monthly values |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Main**: the route of the first release (`docs/METHODS.md` M1.2; decisions D69, D84 and D101 of issue #77, USER-G) | `compute_soil_loss` (EP1) | Yes | The mean annual level, the same for every agricultural year: the variation between years is deferred (USER-G, D69) | Through `c_factor/` for each agricultural year (M1.5, D45, PC-1, D129), each month weighted by its climatological fraction from the twelve monthly records (D84, D101); when a record of it is missing, the declared `rusle_c_factor` for every year (`DECLARED`; M1.2, D125), the PROPOSED fallback of brief #218 | `R` of the month, the level times the climatological fraction of the month (`r_factor/climatology.py`, twelve records), times `K`, `LS`, `C` of its agricultural year and `P` |
| **Daily index**, kept available off the main path | `compute_soil_loss_daily_index` (same signature) | No | Through the daily erosivity index of `r_factor/`: monthly fractions and yearly ratios of the `precipitation` series | Through `c_factor/` (D45, PC-1) for each agricultural year, from the crop and the last harvest of each day of the calendar | `NOT_AVAILABLE` (OPEN 20) |

The daily-index route is what the brief of #173 asked for. It stays off the main path because D69, D84 and USER-G choose the climatology for the first release while S5 and S6 stay behind paywalls (OPEN 11, OPEN 12). Unit `l0/erosion-c-main-route` does not change it: it still reads the canopy records by crop, one record each, and the residue coefficient with the empty scope (OPEN 37).

## Signature

```text
compute_soil_loss(
    calendar: RotationCalendar,
    climate: SiteClimate,
    soil_slope: SiteSoilSlope,
    *,
    parameters: dict[str, dict],
) -> SoilLossResults | Missing
```

As EP1 is written in `contracts/ENTRYPOINTS.md` (#144): `parameters` is the catalogue as `params.catalogue.load_folder` returns it, passed by keyword (G3, D56). It has **no default**: a call without it raises `TypeError`. It is keyword-only, so a call that passes it by position also raises `TypeError`. `compute_soil_loss_daily_index` has the same signature.

Neither function calls `load_folder` or anything else that reads a file (G1). They call the pure functions `params.catalogue.identity` and `params.catalogue.read_value` on the records they were given, and the pure functions of `r_factor/`, `c_factor/`, `ls.py` and `equation.py`, and do not change `parameters`.

## Parameters EP1 reads

Every record is looked up by its `id` and **exactly** the scope below, through `params.catalogue.identity` (case and spacing ignored, as the catalogue compares them). Identifiers are those `LS.md`, `r_factor/README.md` and `c_factor/README.md` propose where they propose one; the others are proposed here. All are `PROPOSED`; the scopes are `PROPOSED` (OPEN 3, OPEN 22).

The scope column says how a record is looked up:

- `site`: the empty scope `{}`. One record for the field and the method.
- `crop or no crop`: `{"crop": "<crop_id>"}`, the `crop_id` of the calendar component that covers the day; on a day no component covers, `{"management": "no_crop"}` (as `engine/water/EXPOSURE.md` reads it).
- `crop of the last harvest`: `{"crop": "<crop_id>"}`, the `crop_id` of the component whose end date is the last one before the day.
- `crop and stage, or no crop` (main route, D129): `{"crop": "<crop_id>", "stage": "day_<N>"}`, one record for each day of the series by days after planting of the crop of the day, `N` a whole number in ASCII digits; every record with that id and the crop's scope plus a `stage` word belongs to the series (OPEN 29, OPEN 30). On a day no component covers, `{"management": "no_crop"}`, one record.

The route column: `main` (`compute_soil_loss`; `main, months` for the monthly values only; `main, C` for the computed `C` only), `daily` (`compute_soil_loss_daily_index`).

**The records of the computed `C` of the main route** (brief #218), the shape the records of AH703 Tables 5-1 and 5-2 are prepared in. Every one is read with exactly this id, scope and unit string; a crop's scope word is the `crop_id` of the calendar (OPEN 34):

Changes made by unit `l0/erosion-c-main-route` (#218, D129): the main route's `C` reads the canopy records with the scope key `stage` (`day_<N>`), alpha and the decay coefficient by the crop of the last harvest, and the new identifiers `rusle2_residue_yield_ratio`, `rusle2_crop_yield` (by crop) and `mass_per_area_kg_ha_to_lb_acre` (site), all PROPOSED; `rusle_c_factor` is read only by the fallback.

### Reading a unit string (PROPOSED)

- **A slope.** `ls.py` needs the unit of `slope_steepness` and of `s_break` as a `SlopeUnit`. A unit string is read as the `SlopeUnit` whose value is that exact string (`percent`, `ratio`, `angle_degrees`, `angle_radians`); any other string is not read, and `LS` is missing with `ls.REASON_UNIT_NOT_ACCEPTED`. OPEN 4.
- **The inverse of a unit** (daily-index route). `c_factor/` takes, next to each coefficient, the unit whose inverse is the coefficient's unit, and compares it with the unit of the input the coefficient multiplies. A record carries one unit string, so EP1 reads that unit from it: `<u>-1` gives `<u>` when `<u>` is one word (no space, no bracket), for example `day-1` gives `day`; `(<u>)-1` gives `<u>` for any `<u>` that is not blank. Any other string is not read, and `C` is missing with `ls.REASON_UNIT_NOT_ACCEPTED`. `b` of S1 Eq. 6.6 must read as the inverse of `percent` (PC-1). OPEN 21.
- **A fraction.** `c_factor/` reads the canopy cover and the residue cover as fractions from zero to one; the canopy-cover record must carry `dimensionless`. OPEN 21.
- **Time since a harvest** is counted in days of the daily series, unit string `day` (the unit string of `mm day-1`, CQ1). OPEN 21.
- **The twelve monthly records** must carry one unit string between them; otherwise their fractions are not defined and every month is missing with `ls.REASON_UNIT_NOT_ACCEPTED`. Which string is OPEN 11.
- **A conversion** (main route's `C`). The record `mass_per_area_kg_ha_to_lb_acre` carries `<to> per <from>`, one ` per ` between two units that are not blank: `<to>` must be the unit whose inverse alpha carries and `<from>` the unit of the yield. Any other string, or another pair, is a mismatch (OPEN 32).
- **A ratio** (main route's `C`). `rusle2_residue_yield_ratio` must carry `dimensionless`.
- **A series** (main route's `C`). The records of one series carry one unit string.

## What the functions do, in order

1. **Types (G5).** `calendar`, `climate` or `soil_slope` that is not an instance of its model, or `parameters` that is not a `dict` whose keys are `str` and whose items are `dict`, raises `TypeError` naming the argument.
2. **One field (G5).** When `climate` or `soil_slope` names another `case_id` or `field_id` than `calendar`, `ValueError`, naming the identifier fields that differ, never their values.
3. **Start month (EP1, Q4).** When `calendar.ag_year_start.month` is missing: one `Missing`, reason `MISSING_INPUT`, `detail` names the start month.
4. **Precipitation series (G4, third row; D47).** When `climate.daily_series` has no item with quantity `precipitation`: one `Missing`, reason `MISSING_INPUT`, `detail` names `precipitation`.
5. **Years and months (C5.3, D47).** From the agricultural year that holds the series' `start_date` to the one that holds its `end_date`, each with its twelve calendar months starting at `calendar.ag_year_start.month` in calendar year `ag_year`.
6. **When the calendar is feasible**, once for the call: the rotation repeated over the years of the record (`engine/calendar/repeat.py`, D47), then the factors of the route (below).
7. **Reason or number of each value**, the first row that applies:

| Situation | Annual value | Monthly value | Convention |
|---|---|---|---|
| The calendar is infeasible | `NOT_COMPUTABLE` | `NOT_COMPUTABLE` | G4, first row |
| The series does not cover the year (or the month) from its first day to its last | `NO_COVERAGE` | `NO_COVERAGE` (PROPOSED: C5.5 speaks of yearly values) | C5.5, OPEN 5 |
| Every other value | The route's annual value, or its `Missing` | The route's monthly value, or its `Missing` | G4 |

Every value keeps `indicator`, `direction` and `unit` (C3.1), taken from `contracts/results/models.py`. A number carries `provenance` `DERIVED` (C4.6), a `source`, no `range` and `range_missing` with reason `NOT_AVAILABLE`: how a range passes through the product is not built (C2.3, OPEN 5 of `EQUATION.md`).

## The main route (`compute_soil_loss`)

### The annual value

`equation.annual_soil_loss(r=..., k=..., ls=..., c=..., p=..., accepted_units=ACCEPTED_UNITS, unit_guard_reason=ls.REASON_UNIT_NOT_ACCEPTED)` of:

- `R`: the record `rusle_r_factor`, the mean annual level, for every agricultural year (USER-G, D69: the variation between years is deferred);
- `K`, `P`: one record each;
- `LS`: through `ls.py` (below);
- `C`: `C` of the agricultural year, [computed](#c-of-the-agricultural-year-d129) through `c_factor/` (M1.5, D45, PC-1, D129); or, when a record it needs is missing, the record `rusle_c_factor` for every year, the single declared value (`DECLARED`; M1.2, D69, D84, D101, D125), by the PROPOSED fallback of brief #218. When the declared record cannot be read either, its detail says so after the fallback text.

As `EQUATION.md` describes it: `MISSING_INPUT` naming every missing factor (`factor '<name>' is missing (reason <CODE>: <detail>)`); then the unit guard (a factor in another unit string than the accepted one); then `NOT_COMPUTABLE` for a product that is not finite. With the fallback the value is the same for every covered year; with the computed `C` it changes with `C` from year to year. Its `source` names the level of `R`, which path `C` took (the computed `C` with every record it read and its scope, or the declared `C` with the words `DECLARED` and `PROPOSED fallback of brief #218`, D125, USER-G and every record of the computed `C` that is missing), `LS` from `ls.py`, `K` and `P`, and every record read with its scope.

### C of the agricultural year (D129)

Decision D129 of issue #77 sets the design, brief #218 the fallback; the rest is PROPOSED. `C` is computed for every agricultural year of the record (a month can be covered in a year that is not), from the calendar and the records, never from the series.

1. **Every record is read first, over every year**: the twelve monthly records; the site records `rusle2_canopy_fall_height_coefficient` and `rusle2_surface_cover_b_per_percent`; for each crop on the field on some day, its canopy series (below); for a day with no crop, the two records of `{"management": "no_crop"}`; for each crop of a last harvest, `rusle2_residue_yield_ratio`, `rusle2_crop_yield`, `rusle2_residue_decay_coefficient` and `rusle2_residue_mass_cover_coefficient` by that crop, and the conversion record when the yield is not in the unit whose inverse alpha carries.
2. **The fallback** (PROPOSED, brief #218). When any of these records is missing (no record, a `null` value, a record `read_value` refuses, a record that cannot be compared, or a series with no record at all), `C` of every year is the declared `rusle_c_factor`, and the source and any detail say so and name each missing record by id and scope. It applies to the whole call, so one rotation's years never mix the two paths (OPEN 31).
3. **Otherwise each year on its own.** Any other problem makes `C` of that year missing, naming each problem and saying that the fallback does not apply: a day whose crop or last harvest is not known (as on the daily-index route, OPEN 2); a stage word that is not `day_<N>` or two words for one day (`NOT_COMPUTABLE`); records whose units disagree (`ls.REASON_UNIT_NOT_ACCEPTED`, naming the records involved): a series in more than one unit string, a canopy cover that is not `dimensionless`, a ratio that is not `dimensionless`, alpha or the decay coefficient not read as an inverse, `b` not `percent-1`, a conversion whose `<to> per <from>` is not the unit of alpha and the unit of the yield; and the monthly records in more than one unit string, or fractions that `r_factor/` refuses.
4. **The canopy of a day.** The days after planting are the days from the start date of the component that covers the day, in the repeated rotation (D47), to the day; the canopy cover and fall height are read on the crop's series at that number (OPEN 29): linear between two days of the series, the value of the first day before it, the value of the last day after it. A day with no crop reads the one record of `{"management": "no_crop"}`.
5. **The residue of a day.** The last harvest before the day, its crop `h`: the residue mass at harvest is `ratio(h) * yield(h)`, times the conversion when the yield needs one, in the unit whose inverse `alpha(h)` carries; it decays from the end date with the decay coefficient of `h`; its cover is `residue_cover_from_mass` with `alpha(h)`. Older residue is not added (OPEN 23).
6. **The soil-loss ratio of a day**, as on the daily-index route under PC-1: `surface_cover_subfactor_percent`, `effective_canopy_cover`, `canopy_subfactor`, `soil_loss_ratio`. The first refusal of `c_factor/` makes `C` of the year missing, naming the day.
7. **`C` of the year**: the mean ratio of each month (OPEN 24), weighted by the climatological fraction of its calendar month from the twelve monthly records (`r_factor.climatological_monthly_fractions`; D84, D101) through `rotation_c` with one year (OPEN 25).

### The monthly values (PROPOSED)

1. The twelve records `rusle_r_monthly_erosivity_<month>` are read. Any that cannot be read makes every month missing (`MISSING_INPUT`), naming each by id and scope with `needed by the climatological monthly fraction of R of docs/METHODS.md M1.2 (decisions D69, D84 and D101 of issue #77, USER-G; engine/erosion/r_factor/climatology.py)`. Records in more than one unit string make every month missing with `ls.REASON_UNIT_NOT_ACCEPTED`.
2. `r_factor.climatological_monthly_fractions` of the twelve numbers, then `r_factor.climatological_r_by_year_and_month(mean_annual_r=<level>, mean_annual_r_unit=<its unit>, fractions, ag_years=<every year of the record>, start_month)`: `R` of the month is the level times the fraction of its calendar month. Their refusals are theirs (a month below zero, twelve that add up to zero, a level in another unit).
3. A covered month is `R` of the month times `K`, `LS`, `C` of its agricultural year and `P`, unit `t ha-1 month-1`; the twelve months of a year add up to the annual value (C1.2). It is missing when the annual product is (its detail repeated as `its factors are those of the annual product: ...`) or when `R` of the month is, naming both; the reason is `MISSING_INPUT` when either is a missing input.

When any monthly record is missing from `parameters`, every month is `MISSING_INPUT`, naming each missing record (D56), with D69, D84 and D101 in the detail (since `#203`, the twelve GloREDa records for the demonstration point are in `params/records/`; OPEN 11).

## The daily-index route (`compute_soil_loss_daily_index`), off the main path

### `R` through `r_factor/` (M1.2)

1. The records of the level, the exponent and the threshold are read. When the exponent or the threshold cannot be read, the monthly erosivity fractions are missing (`MISSING_INPUT`, `the monthly erosivity fractions: ...`), and so is `R` (`R by month: ...`); each names the record by id and scope and adds `needed by the daily erosivity index of docs/METHODS.md M1.2 (engine/erosion/r_factor/), whose monthly fractions and yearly ratios R and C need`. A level that cannot be read makes `R` missing the same way and leaves the fractions.
2. Otherwise `r_factor.r_factor_parts(...)`. Every refusal is `r_factor/`'s: a series or a threshold not in `mm day-1`, a level not in `MJ mm ha-1 h-1 yr-1`, a missing day (that day, its month and its agricultural year missing, never zero rain), a year without an erosive day (its fractions `NOT_COMPUTABLE`).
3. `R` of a covered agricultural year is the sum of its twelve values of `R` by month. Every month that is not a number is named, never dropped: a month missing in the table of `r_factor/` once per reason and detail; a month the table does not hold, or holds as something other than a number, as `NOT_COMPUTABLE`. The reason is the one the named months share, otherwise `MISSING_INPUT`. The same holds for the monthly fractions `C` reads.

### `C` through `c_factor/` (M1.5, D45, PC-1)

`C` is computed for each covered agricultural year on its own, over its twelve months, with one year (PROPOSED, OPEN 25).

1. **Crop of a day** (PROPOSED, as `engine/water/`): the `crop_id` of the one component of the rotation repeated over the record (D47, `engine/calendar/repeat.py`) whose dates cover the day; `{"management": "no_crop"}` when no component can cover it, also between the end of one cycle and the start of the next. Not known (`MISSING_INPUT`) for a day before the first cycle when the cycle before the first is not laid out, because none of its components has an end date on or after the first day of the record's years (the text `BEFORE_FIRST_CYCLE`, naming D47, D141, point 13 of `engine/calendar/repeat.py` and OPEN 2), and when a component with a missing date could cover the day, from its cycle's start to the day before the next cycle starts (point 7 of `engine/calendar/repeat.py`); `NOT_COMPUTABLE` when two dated components cover it. The periods of a component are not read (OPEN 22).
2. **Residue of a day** (PROPOSED, OPEN 23): the residue left by the last harvest before the day, in the repeated rotation. Not known (`MISSING_INPUT`) when a component without an end date could have ended before the day and after every known end before it, and when no component laid out has an end date before the day (the text `NO_HARVEST_BEFORE`: the days up to the first harvest that is laid out; a harvest before the first day of the record's years is not laid out, and of the cycle before the first only the components that can reach that day are laid out, so the residue left before the record stays missing; it names D47, D141, point 13 of `engine/calendar/repeat.py`, OPEN 2 and OPEN 40); `NOT_COMPUTABLE` when two components end on that last date. Older residue is not added.
3. **Every problem is gathered first**: the monthly erosivity fractions of the year, the crop and the residue of every day (each text once, with the number of days and the first day), and every record that cannot be read (by id and scope, with `needed by <the part of M1.5 that needs it>`). Any problem makes `C` missing: `C of agricultural year <Y> is not computed: ...`, reason `MISSING_INPUT` (C3.3), or the reason of the first problem when none is an input that is missing. Then the unit strings: any that is not read makes `C` missing with `ls.REASON_UNIT_NOT_ACCEPTED`, naming each.
4. **The soil-loss ratio of each day**, through `c_factor/`, under PC-1:
   - `residue_mass_after_decay(...)` of the mass of the last harvest, its residue type that crop, `elapsed_time` the days since its end date in `day`;
   - `residue_cover_from_mass(...)` of that mass;
   - `surface_cover_subfactor_percent(surface_residue_cover=<that cover>, surface_cover_coefficient_per_percent=<b>)` (S1 Eq. 6.6, roughness term at one);
   - `effective_canopy_cover(canopy_cover=<record of the crop of the day>, ground_cover=<that cover>)` (S1 Eq. 6.5);
   - `canopy_subfactor(canopy_cover=<the effective cover>, fall_height, ...)`;
   - `soil_loss_ratio(canopy=..., surface_cover=...)`.

   The first refusal of a day makes `C` missing with the reason `c_factor/` gives: `C of agricultural year <Y> is not computed: on <date>, <function> of engine/erosion/c_factor/ ...: <its detail>`.
5. **The soil-loss ratio of a month** is the mean of the ratios of its days (PROPOSED, OPEN 24), passed to `rotation_c` as a `c_factor.SoilLossRatio` whose `canopy` and `surface_cover` are the means of the two sub-factors over the days.
6. **`rotation_c`** over the twelve months of the year, each with its erosivity fraction, `number_of_years=1`, and `fraction_sum_tolerance` missing with `NOT_AVAILABLE` (OPEN 27).

The annual value is `equation.annual_soil_loss` of `R` and `C` of the year, `K`, `LS` and `P`, as on the main route. Every covered month is `NOT_AVAILABLE` (OPEN 20). The `source` says the value comes from the daily-index route, off the main path.

## Numbers in the modules

`soil_loss.py` holds two integers, `12` and `1`, and no other number. `_parameters.py` holds one, the `1` of "more than one record". A test reads both modules and fails on any other number, and on any format specification in an f-string of `soil_loss.py`. The month of a monthly erosivity record is a word in its identifier (`january` to `december`), not a number.

| Integer | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `12` | `soil_loss.py`, `_MONTHS_IN_A_YEAR` | Twelve calendar months to a year and to an agricultural year (C5.1, C5.3) |
| `1` | `soil_loss.py`, `_FIRST` | Months and days are counted from one (C5.1); "more than one" component, unit string or reason |
| `1` | `soil_loss.py`, `_ONE_AGRICULTURAL_YEAR` | `C` of one agricultural year is `rotation_c` over one year (OPEN 25) |
| `1` | `_parameters.py` | More than one record with one id and scope |

The test file builds its PLACEHOLDER numbers as `PLACEHOLDER_BASE` plus the position in the list of identifiers, a base plainly unreal, far above any value a factor or a coefficient of RUSLE takes. The records whose forms need a number of a certain size to give any result (the exponent and the threshold of the daily index, the coefficients of `c_factor/`, and the crop records: a canopy cover is a fraction) carry repeated digits (`1.11`, `0.555`), as `engine/water/test_exposure.py` does. The daily precipitation of a computable series repeats `RAIN`, repeated digits and zero, plainly not weather. None is a value of the method. Every record carries the word PLACEHOLDER in its citation, persons and reasons, and is passed as an in-memory copy in which the word is replaced by `UNMARKED_TEST_ONLY`, as `params/test_layout.py` does (question 12 of `params/README.md`); that copy passes `check_catalogue`.

The expected values are computed again in the test from the forms, without calling the module: on the main route from the records; on the daily-index route day by day, from the PROPOSED forms of `r_factor/README.md` and `c_factor/README.md` under PC-1 and the calendar the test built, repeated over the record by the form of `engine/calendar/repeat.py` computed again in the test (`repeated_windows`).

The residue cover at sowing, the most sensitive input of M1.5, is not applied as such, and the result is not shown for more than one level (one call is one scenario; USER-AA withdrew the generic levels). Whether the residue comes from the residue ledger is Q12 of `contracts/ENTRYPOINTS.md`. For the lead developer and the science reviewers.
24. **The soil-loss ratio of a month** (daily-index route). PROPOSED: the mean of the daily ratios over the days of the month, a declared partial stage of the daily route of RUSLE2 (PC-3, USER-L). The alternatives are to weight each day by its erosivity, or to integrate daily. For the science reviewers.
25. **`C` by agricultural year** (daily-index route). PROPOSED: `rotation_c` over the twelve months of each agricultural year with one year, so `C` changes from year to year and a year whose fractions are missing leaves only that year missing. M1.5 divides by "the number of years of the rotation", which would give one `C` over all the years. For the lead developer and the science reviewers.
26. **The control regression of M1.2.** Out of both routes (PC-7): its two constants are not read and no comparison reaches the result.
27. **The tolerance of the sum of the fractions** (daily-index route). EP1 passes it to `rotation_c` as missing (`NOT_AVAILABLE`), because no person has supplied it (OPEN 9 of `c_factor/README.md`); `C` does not need it. For the lead developer and the science reviewers.

What stays open is listed from OPEN 29 on.
29. **How a series by days after planting is read** (D129 (a) leaves it to this unit to propose). PROPOSED: linear in the days after planting between two days of the series; before its first day, the value of its first day; after its last day and up to the harvest, the value of its last day; a series of one record holds on every day. The alternatives: from zero cover at planting to the first day (it writes a value no record holds), a step function, or no value beyond the last day (a missing `C` for long seasons). The days after planting count from the start date of the component, the periods of the calendar are not read. For the lead developer.
30. **Which days a series must have.** Every record with the id and the crop's scope plus a `stage` word belongs to the series; a series with no record is missing (the fallback), but a series that lacks some of the days of its table is read from the days it has, because EP1 does not know which days the table prints. Whether the catalogue check should require the days 15, 30, ... up to the last tabulated day of each crop is for the lead developer, who owns `params/`.
31. **The extent of the fallback.** PROPOSED: one missing record makes `C` of every year of the call the declared record (one rotation never mixes the two paths), and only a record that is missing leads to it. A unit mismatch, a stage word not read, a day without a known crop or last harvest, or a refusal of `c_factor/` makes `C` of the year missing, with no fallback, so that an error in the records is shown, not hidden by a number. Whether those should fall back too, and whether the fallback should be per year, is for the lead developer.
32. **Unit strings of the residue chain.** PROPOSED: alpha carries the inverse of the mass per area as `(<M>)-1`, for example `(lb acre-1)-1`, the form EP1 reads for the inverse of a unit of more than one word; the spelling `acre lb-1` of AH703 is not read. The conversion record carries `<M> per <Y>`, for example `lb acre-1 per kg ha-1`; strings are compared exactly, no unit is converted in code. The identifiers `rusle2_residue_yield_ratio`, `rusle2_crop_yield` and `mass_per_area_kg_ha_to_lb_acre` are PROPOSED (D129 (c) asks for them to be proposed); the id of the conversion, given by the orchestrator session for yields in `kg ha-1` and alpha per `lb acre-1`, names those units, but EP1 reads the units from its unit string, not from the id, and reads it for any yield unit that is not the unit of alpha (one conversion record per catalogue). For the lead developer (the identifiers, the reading) and the science reviewers (the units).
