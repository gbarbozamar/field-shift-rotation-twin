# Withdrawn records

Records taken out of the parameter catalogue and kept. The record format is described in [../README.md](../README.md), and so is the rule of this folder (section "Files in `withdrawn/`", PROPOSED): the service loads `params/records/` only, so no record of this folder reaches an engine through the service. Each file is still checked as a record by `python -m pytest params/`.

No record was edited when it was moved here. Each file is the file that was checked and signed (D34), byte for byte: its value, unit, range, citation, note, `transcribed_by` and `checked_by`.

## `computed_c/`: 61 records of the computed `C`

The folder holds 61 of the 67 records of the computed `C` of the main route of EP1 (D129, unit l0/c-records), in the shapes of brief #218: the ones that only the computed `C` reads. They are the canopy cover and fall-height series of AH703 Table 5-2 by days after planting and the decay coefficient and mass-cover coefficient of AH703 Table 5-1, for the crops `soybean`, `wheat` (spring small grain, declared) and `maize`; the no-crop canopy (declared 0); and the site records (fall-height coefficient, the declared b, the kg ha-1 to lb acre-1 conversion). The other six, the residue/yield ratio of AH703 Table 5-1 and the DIEA yield (D131) of the three crops, are in `params/records/residues/`, inside the catalogue, because the residue ledger (EP4) reads them too. The crop words must be the `crop_id` of the calendars. Known: with the 67 records the computed `C` of soybean with winter fallow is about 0.53, far above the 0.036 measured for continuous no-till cropping in Uruguay (D125): soil biomass, consolidation and roughness are set to one (D45, PC-3) and only the residue of the last harvest counts.

**Why they are here.** [D148](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6888128333) withdraws the computed `C` from the demonstration after the check of EP1 against the soil loss measured on the La Estanzuela runoff plots (not passed for the computed `C`; the entry [EP1: plausibility against the La Estanzuela runoff plots](../../docs/VALIDATION.md#ep1-plausibility-against-the-la-estanzuela-runoff-plots-20172019) of `docs/VALIDATION.md`). By D148 the code of the computed `C` (#229) and its signed records (#237) stay in the repository as a documented capability that is not validated. Decision [D158](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889049425) of issue #77 (lead developer, 15 November 2026) says where the withdrawal takes place: in the catalogue the service loads. [D164](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889521276) amends D158: "The six records the residue ledger reads stay in the catalogue the service loads; the other 61 leave." In its words, D148 withdrew the computed `C` from the demonstration; it did not withdraw the residue ledger. While the 67 records were under `params/records/computed_c/`, the service loaded them, and EP1 computed `C` from them for the two rotations of D128 instead of taking the declared `C` of D125. Unit l0/computed-c-out-of-catalogue moved the 61 here and the six to `params/records/residues/`, with `git mv`.

**What follows.** With the catalogue of `params/records/` alone, EP1 finds six of the records of the computed `C` and not the other 61, so it cannot compute `C`: it takes the declared record `rusle_c_factor` (D125) by its fallback for every rotation and every covered year, and says so in the source of each value, naming the records that are missing. D158 states the consequence and accepts it: with the declared `C`, the soil loss at the demonstration point is the same figure for both rotations of D128, so in the demonstration erosion does not separate the rotations. The residue ledger reads its six records as before, so its result does not change. EP1 itself is unchanged, and no record value changes.

**The capability stays tested.** `engine/erosion/test_soil_loss.py` loads this folder by its path, adds its records in memory to the catalogue of `params/records/`, which holds the other six, and checks that EP1 still computes `C` from the 67 for both rotations of D128. `engine/erosion/test_records_shared_with_residues.py` checks that the six are in the catalogue and that the residue ledger with the catalogue is the ledger with all 67. A record moved back under `params/records/` is loaded by the service again.
