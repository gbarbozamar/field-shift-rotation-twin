# Withdrawn records

Records taken out of the parameter catalogue and kept. The record format is described in [../README.md](../README.md), and so is the rule of this folder (section "Files in `withdrawn/`", PROPOSED): the service loads `params/records/` only, so no record of this folder reaches an engine through the service. Each file is still checked as a record by `python -m pytest params/`.

No record was edited when it was moved here. Each file is the file that was checked and signed (D34), byte for byte: its value, unit, range, citation, note, `transcribed_by` and `checked_by`.

## `computed_c/`: the 67 records of the computed `C`

The folder holds the 67 records of the computed `C` of the main route of EP1 (D129, unit l0/c-records), in the shapes of brief #218: the canopy cover and fall-height series of AH703 Table 5-2 by days after planting, the residue/yield ratio, decay coefficient and mass-cover coefficient of AH703 Table 5-1, and the DIEA yields (D131) for the crops `soybean`, `wheat` (spring small grain, declared) and `maize`; the no-crop canopy (declared 0); and the site records (fall-height coefficient, the declared b, the kg ha-1 to lb acre-1 conversion). The crop words must be the `crop_id` of the calendars. Known: with these records the computed `C` of soybean with winter fallow is about 0.53, far above the 0.036 measured for continuous no-till cropping in Uruguay (D125): soil biomass, consolidation and roughness are set to one (D45, PC-3) and only the residue of the last harvest counts.

**Why they are here.** [D148](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6888128333) withdraws the computed `C` from the demonstration after the check of EP1 against the soil loss measured on the La Estanzuela runoff plots (not passed for the computed `C`; the entry [EP1: plausibility against the La Estanzuela runoff plots](../../docs/VALIDATION.md#ep1-plausibility-against-the-la-estanzuela-runoff-plots-20172019) of `docs/VALIDATION.md`). By D148 the code of the computed `C` (#229) and its signed records (#237) stay in the repository as a documented capability that is not validated. Decision [D158](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889049425) of issue #77 (lead developer, 15 November 2026) says where the withdrawal takes place: in the catalogue the service loads. While these records were under `params/records/computed_c/`, the service loaded them, and EP1 computed `C` from them for the two rotations of D128 instead of taking the declared `C` of D125. Unit l0/computed-c-out-of-catalogue moved them here with `git mv`.

**What follows.** With the catalogue of `params/records/` alone, EP1 finds no record of the computed `C`, takes the declared record `rusle_c_factor` (D125) by its fallback for every rotation and every covered year, and says so in the source of each value. D158 states the consequence and accepts it: with the declared `C`, the soil loss at the demonstration point is the same figure for both rotations of D128, so in the demonstration erosion does not separate the rotations. EP1 itself is unchanged, and no record value changes.

**The capability stays tested.** `engine/erosion/test_soil_loss.py` loads this folder by its path, adds its records to the catalogue of `params/records/` in memory, and checks that EP1 still computes `C` from them for both rotations of D128. A record moved back under `params/records/` is loaded by the service again.
