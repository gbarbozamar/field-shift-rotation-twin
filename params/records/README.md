# Parameter records

One JSON file per parameter record. The format is described in [../README.md](../README.md).

Values, ranges and citations follow decision D34 of issue #77: an AI session may transcribe them from a published source with the full reference, the locator, the verbatim text and the date read, and one person checks every number against the source and signs `checked_by`. A value that no source prints (a declared value, such as the single C or P = 1) is declared by the lead developer, and its record says so in `note`.

This folder is the catalogue: the service loads every record under it, subfolders included, and passes them to the engines. Today it holds 71 records: 26 at its top and 45 in `water/`.

At its top are the 26 records of the soil-loss value at the demonstration point La Estanzuela (unit l0/first-number): the ten LS coefficients, R of Colonia (D83), K of the class T Brunosol (D100), the declared C and P, and the twelve monthly erosivity values of GloREDa at the point (D84, D101). R, K, C and P carry the scope `{}` because EP1 looks them up with the empty scope; reading a site scope is a later unit (PROPOSED). The values for one site in the empty scope are a known limit: a second site needs a scope key that EP1 reads. K is also in `data/demo/la_estanzuela_soil_slope.json`, which EP1 does not read today: a known redundancy.

The 67 records of the computed `C` of the main route of EP1 (D129, unit l0/c-records) are not in this folder any more. Unit l0/computed-c-out-of-catalogue moved them, unchanged, from `computed_c/` here to [`../withdrawn/computed_c/`](../withdrawn/README.md): [D148](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6888128333) withdraws the computed `C` from the demonstration, and decision [D158](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889049425) of issue #77 says where: in the catalogue the service loads. Without them EP1 takes the declared `C` above (D125) for every rotation, by its fallback, and the source of each value says so.

The subfolder `water/` holds 45 records of the water engine (EP3), each checked by the lead developer against its source and signed (D34, D66; unit l0/water-records-signed), copied unchanged from the signed drafts. All cite FAO-56 (Allen et al. 1998, official HTML edition) at the locator of the record: the 31 constants of the reference evapotranspiration (chapters 2 and 3; identifiers `<group>_<field>` of `engine/water/et0/constants.py`), the two bounds of PC-4 (D82; `net_longwave_relative_shortwave_maximum` and `penman_monteith_minimum_wind_speed`), and the 12 basal crop coefficients of Table 17 (`basal_crop_coefficient`, scope `crop` and `stage`: `initial`, `mid`, `end` for `soybean`, `wheat`, `maize` and `alfalfa`). The 33 records of the method carry the scope `{}`. The crop words must be the `crop_id` of the calendars; for wheat the note of each record states the row of Table 17 the lead developer chose. Known: these records alone give no water value. The other water records EP3 reads (runoff, surface layer, upper limit of the crop coefficient, root depth, depletion fraction, stage lengths, the soil column) are not signed and are not in the catalogue, so every year stays missing with its reason, naming them.
