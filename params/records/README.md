# Parameter records

One JSON file per parameter record. The format is described in [../README.md](../README.md).

Values, ranges and citations follow decision D34 of issue #77: an AI session may transcribe them from a published source with the full reference, the locator, the verbatim text and the date read, and one person checks every number against the source and signs `checked_by`. A value that no source prints (a declared value, such as the single C or P = 1) is declared by the lead developer, and its record says so in `note`.

This folder is the catalogue: the service loads every record under it, subfolders included, and passes them to the engines. Today it holds 132 records: 26 at its top, 100 in `water/` and 6 in `residues/` (counted by a script over the `.json` files of each folder; `load_folder` reads 132 and `check_catalogue` reports no problem).

At its top are the 26 records of the soil-loss value at the demonstration point La Estanzuela (unit l0/first-number): the ten LS coefficients, R of Colonia (D83), K of the class T Brunosol (D100), the declared C and P, and the twelve monthly erosivity values of GloREDa at the point (D84, D101). R, K, C and P carry the scope `{}` because EP1 looks them up with the empty scope; reading a site scope is a later unit (PROPOSED). The values for one site in the empty scope are a known limit: a second site needs a scope key that EP1 reads. K is also in `data/demo/la_estanzuela_soil_slope.json`, which EP1 does not read today: a known redundancy.

The subfolder `residues/` holds the six records the residue ledger (EP4, `engine/residues/`) reads for the aboveground residue at harvest, the residue/yield ratio times the yield (D129 (c)): the residue/yield ratio of AH703 Table 5-1 (`rusle2_residue_yield_ratio`) and the DIEA yield (`rusle2_crop_yield`, D131) for the crops `soybean`, `wheat` (the row "Wheat (spring)", D142) and `maize` (unit l0/c-records). The crop words must be the `crop_id` of the calendars. They are six of the 67 records of the computed `C` of the main route of EP1 (D129), which lay under `computed_c/` here. Unit l0/computed-c-out-of-catalogue moved the other 61, unchanged, to [`../withdrawn/computed_c/`](../withdrawn/README.md), and these six, unchanged, to `residues/`: [D148](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6888128333) withdraws the computed `C` from the demonstration, decision [D158](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889049425) of issue #77 says where, in the catalogue the service loads, and [D164](https://github.com/gbarbozamar/field-shift-rotation-twin/issues/77#issuecomment-6889521276) amends D158: the six records the residue ledger also reads stay in the catalogue. EP1 reads the six too, but cannot compute `C` from them alone: it takes the declared `C` above (D125) for every rotation, by its fallback, and the source of each value says so.

The subfolder `water/` holds 45 records of the water engine (EP3), each checked by the lead developer against its source and signed (D34, D66; unit l0/water-records-signed), copied unchanged from the signed drafts. All cite FAO-56 (Allen et al. 1998, official HTML edition) at the locator of the record: the 31 constants of the reference evapotranspiration (chapters 2 and 3; identifiers `<group>_<field>` of `engine/water/et0/constants.py`), the two bounds of PC-4 (D82; `net_longwave_relative_shortwave_maximum` and `penman_monteith_minimum_wind_speed`), and the 12 basal crop coefficients of Table 17 (`basal_crop_coefficient`, scope `crop` and `stage`: `initial`, `mid`, `end` for `soybean`, `wheat`, `maize` and `alfalfa`). The 33 records of the method carry the scope `{}`. The crop words must be the `crop_id` of the calendars; for wheat the note of each record states the row of Table 17 the lead developer chose. When they were loaded, these records alone gave no water value: the other water records EP3 reads (runoff, surface layer, upper limit of the crop coefficient, root depth, depletion fraction, stage lengths, the soil column) were not signed and not in the catalogue. The next paragraph says which of them are now.
